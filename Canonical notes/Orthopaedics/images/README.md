> These diagrams, clinical photographs and radiographs are **existing published works**, not AI-generated illustrations. ## Editorial notes

- Each visual is placed near the related concept. Clinical X-rays are examples, **not complete fracture-classification atlases**.
- The hip fracture overview in Chapter 03 indicates anatomical regions; **it does not illustrate the Garden I–IV displacement classification**.
- The previously deleted 24 inaccurate, AI-generated PNGs have **not** been reinstated.
- Licensing and author attribution are provided by the linked Wikimedia Commons source pages, whose rights statements may differ from image to image.
- For fully offline Obsidian use, source originals must first be downloaded, verified, credited, and then substituted with local `![[images/filename.ext]]` embeds.
