## 3. Comparisons that are regularly tested

### Acute embolus vs chronic thrombosis

| Property | **Embolus** | **Thrombosis on PAD** |
|---|---|---|
| Onset | Dramatic, sudden | Often acute on chronic |
| Prior claudication | Often absent | Often present |
| Contralateral limb | Can be relatively healthy | Often PAD signs |
| Cardiac source | AF/valvular/ventricular clot possible | Underlying plaque dominant |
| Collaterals | Less developed | May be developed |
| Revascularization | Fogarty embolectomy and other strategies | Thrombectomy + correction of plaque, endovascular/bypass as indicated |

### Aneurysm vs dissection vs pseudoaneurysm

| Finding | True aneurysm | Pseudoaneurysm | Aortic dissection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall | Dilated full wall | Contained blood outside breached full wall | Blood splits medial layers |
| Imaging | Enlarged vascular lumen/sac | Communicating sac/neck | **Intimal flap; true/false lumen** |
| Causes | Wall degeneration, genetic or inflammatory | Trauma, iatrogenic puncture, anastomosis | HTN or predisposition to medial disease |
| Exam trap | Rupture risk rises with size and other factors | Pulsatile mass after catheterization | Acute chest/back pain and branch ischemia |

### Arterial vs venous ulcer vs neuropathic foot ulcer

| Arterial | Venous | Neuropathic |
|---|---|---|
| **Painful, cold, weak pulses** | **Edematous, warm, pigment** | **Loss of protective sensation** |
| **Distal/toes/lateral** | **Medial gaiter** | **Plantar pressure points** |
| Punched-out deep edge | Sloping shallow edge | Callus around plantar crater |
| Restore blood supply | Compression if vascular supply adequate | Offload, infection/vascular assessment |

### Pneumothorax vs hemothorax vs tamponade
- **Pneumothorax:** ipsilateral absent breath sounds and **hyperresonance**.
- **Hemothorax:** absent breath sounds and **dullness**.
- **Tamponade:** obstructive shock with pericardial fluid, often raised neck veins and muffled heart sounds.
- Always distinguish coexisting hypovolemia; **jugular veins can be flat even in tension physiology if the patient is hemorrhaging**.

### Lung cancer morphology
- **Adenocarcinoma:** peripheral, gland/mucin, TTF-1 and napsin A, driver mutations.
- **Squamous:** central, keratin, p40, PTHrP, may cavitate.
- **Small cell:** central, salt-and-pepper neuroendocrine chromatin, ACTH/ADH/Lambert–Eaton, early metastasis.
- **Large cell:** poorly differentiated large cells, diagnosis of exclusion.
- **Pancoast:** *anatomical syndrome of the apex*, not one unique histology.

## 5. Source conflicts: do not memorize without context

| Source claim | Why it is problematic |
|---|---|
| **Homan/Moses sign diagnoses DVT** | Pain-provocation tests have poor accuracy; use validated clinical probability and ultrasound |
| **Every DVT should receive LMWH for exactly five days then warfarin only** | Anticoagulant choice, overlap and duration vary |
| **Any carotid stenosis ≥70% → angioplasty** | Treatment requires symptomatic status, age, morphology and procedural considerations |
| **Endovascular angioplasty has made bypass obsolete** | Open bypass remains a distinct option for selected anatomy |
| **AAA 5.5 cm is an absolute universal surgery rule** | Sex, growth, anatomy, symptoms and inherited syndromes modify decisions |
| **All aortic dissection gets open graft/EVAR** | Type A and uncomplicated/complicated type B pathways differ |
| **Primary Raynaud never receives pharmacotherapy** | Symptom severity and actual cause govern treatment |
| **Mediastinal thymoma is always the most common tumor** | Depends on age, compartment, primary versus secondary mass and source ranking |
| **Pancoast tumor must be squamous** | Anatomical site/syndrome does not uniquely specify histology |
| **Small cell is the only chemo-responsive lung cancer** | False blanket statement |
| **NSCLC is always operable if early / always inoperable if T4** | Systemic stage, tumor biology, resection complexity and physiologic reserve matter |
| **All pectus anomalies are merely cosmetic** | Severe excavatum may impair functional reserve |
| **Small cell ectopic ACTH causes hypocalcemia** | Source transcription confused the paraneoplastic syndrome |
| **Classic lung cancer TNM size thresholds must be the latest edition** | Source data are version-specific; staging changes over time |
| **Thoracoscore exact nine-variable mnemonic is a validated clinical calculator** | Doctut transcription is incomplete for precise risk computation |
