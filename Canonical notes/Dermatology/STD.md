- Painless

**VDRL: negative** → coz had not entered blood

**Secondary Stage**
- Acral Scaly rash (non itchy)

- Snail track mouth ulcer (mucous patch)
↓
**Latent Stage**
- Antibody still positive- VDRL positive
- Antigen is sleeping
