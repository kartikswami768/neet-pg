| Adverse reaction | Time of onset | Features | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acute dystonia | 1 to 5 days | Spasm of muscles of tongue, face, neck, back | Diphenhydramine or benztropine |
| Akathisia | 5 to 60 days | Restlessness | Propranolol, lorazepam, diphenhydramine, benztropine |

| Neuroleptic malignant syndrome | Weeks to months | Extreme rigidity, fever, unstable blood pressure | Dantrolene |
