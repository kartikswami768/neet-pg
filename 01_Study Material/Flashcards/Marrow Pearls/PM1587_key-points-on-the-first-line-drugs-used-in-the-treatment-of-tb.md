# Key points on the first line drugs used in the treatment of TB

Pearl ID: PM1587

#flashcards

**What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Key points on the first line drugs used in the treatment of TB" (PM1587)?**

?

| Drug | Mechanism of action | Location of Mycobacteria | Metabolism | Unique side effects | Mechanism of resistance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Isoniazid** | Bactericidal; blocks mycolic acid synthesis | Intracellular + extracellular | Liver | Shoulder-hand syndrome and CNS side effects | **katG** mutation — stated as the most severe form of resistance; **inhA** overexpression confers cross-resistance with ethionamide |
| **Rifamycins** | Bactericidal (maximum); inhibit RNA polymerase, leading to inhibition of RNA synthesis | Intracellular + extracellular | Liver (maximum) | Orange urine, sweat and tears; flu-like symptoms with intermittent dosing | Mutation in **rpoB**, the gene coding for the β-subunit of RNA polymerase |
| **Pyrazinamide** | Bactericidal; inhibition of fatty acid synthase-I, leading to inhibition of the cell membrane | Intracellular | Liver | Most hepatotoxic drug; also causes hyperuricemia | Mutation of **pncA** gene |
| **Ethambutol** | Bacteriostatic; inhibits arabinosyl transferase, reducing arabinogalactan needed for the cell wall | Extracellular | Kidney | Optic neuritis; hence not preferred in children | Mutations in **embB** gene |
