# Types of muscle relaxants

Pearl ID: PM0215

#review

**What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Types of muscle relaxants" (PM0215)?**

?

There are 2 types of muscle relaxants: depolarizing and non-depolarizing.

### Depolarizing muscle relaxant
- Succinylcholine

### Non-depolarizing muscle relaxants
- **Ultra short-acting (<10 minutes):** Gantacurium
- **Short-acting (10-20 minutes):** Mivacurium
- **Intermediate-acting (20-50 minutes):** Atracurium, Cisatracurium, Vecuronium, Rocuronium
- **Long-acting (>50 minutes):** Pancuronium, Doxacurium, Pipecuronium
