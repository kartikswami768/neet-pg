# Acute bacterial meningitis - causes and empirical treatment

Pearl ID: PM2433

#flashcards

**What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Acute bacterial meningitis - causes and empirical treatment" (PM2433)?**

?

| Patient group | Bacterial pathogens | Empirical therapy |
|---|---|---|
| Age < 1 month | *E. coli*; Group B Streptococci; Enterobacteriaceae; *Listeria monocytogenes* | 3rd-generation cephalosporin + Ampicillin |
| 1 month–2 years | *E. coli*; Group B Streptococci; *H. influenzae*; *S. pneumoniae*; *N. meningitidis* | 3rd-generation cephalosporin + Ampicillin + Vancomycin (if PRSP suspected) |
| 2–50 years and no risk factors for Listeria | *S. pneumoniae*; *N. meningitidis* | 3rd-generation cephalosporin + Vancomycin |
| Age > 50 years and/or risk factors for Listeria | *S. pneumoniae*; *N. meningitidis*; *L. monocytogenes*; Group B Streptococci | 3rd-generation cephalosporin + Vancomycin + Ampicillin |
| Post-craniotomy / head trauma / CSF rhinorrhea | Gram-negative bacilli; *S. aureus* | Ceftazidime + Vancomycin |
| Post-shunt surgery / Ommaya reservoir | Coagulase-negative staphylococci | Ceftazidime + Vancomycin |
| Immunocompromised state | *S. pneumoniae*; *N. meningitidis*; *L. monocytogenes*; Gram-negative bacilli | Vancomycin + Ampicillin + Cefepime **or** Vancomycin + Meropenem |

**PRSP:** penicillin-resistant *S. pneumoniae*.
