**What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Classification of local anesthetics based on chemical structure" (PM0415)?**

?

| Feature | Amino ester | Amino amide |
|---|---|---|
| Stability | Relatively unstable in solution | Relatively more stable due to the presence of methoxy group |
| Metabolism | Plasma cholinesterase; exception: cocaine - predominantly by hepatic carboxylesterase | Hepatic metabolism (amidase); exception: articaine - plasma cholinesterase |
| Duration of action | Shorter-acting; exception: tetracaine | Longer-acting; exception: articaine |
| Allergic reaction | High incidence due to metabolite PABA | Rare |
| Examples | Procaine; Chloroprocaine; Cocaine; Benzocaine; Tetracaine | Lidocaine; Mepivacaine; Prilocaine; Bupivacaine; Ropivacaine; Etidocaine; Dibucaine; Articaine |
