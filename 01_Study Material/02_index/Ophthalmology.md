Syllabus-Wise Topic List (Cornea & Ocular Surface — NEET PG / INI-CET Pattern)

## Anatomy, Physiology & Biochemistry of Cornea
- Corneal transparency, glycosaminoglycans (Keratan sulfate)
- Corneal hydration state, pump-leak mechanism, and role of Endothelium

## Ocular Surface Diagnostics & Staining Techniques
- Dyes used in corneal/ocular surface evaluation (Fluorescein, Rose Bengal, Lissamine Green) and correct sequence of application
- Optical filters used in slit-lamp examination (Cobalt Blue filter for Fluorescein)

## Infectious Keratitis (Corneal Ulcers)
- Fungal Keratitis: Clinical features (feathery margins, satellite lesions, hypopyon, Wessely ring), risk factors (vegetative trauma/trauma with fish tail), causative organisms (Aspergillus, Fusarium), diagnostic stains, and medical management (Natamycin)
- Acanthamoeba Keratitis: Risk factors (contact lens usage/poor hygiene), clinical features (ring infiltrates, severe pain), pseudopodia morphology, and diagnostic stains (Calcofluor White)
- Bacterial & Atypical Keratitis: Bacterial hypopyon ulcer vs Ulcus Serpens, Nocardia keratitis presenting with fungal-like morphology
- Ocular Manifestations of Systemic Infections: Leprosy (lagophthalmos, neuroparalytic keratitis), COVID-19 ocular surface manifestations

## Corneal Dystrophies & Endothelial Disorders
- Epithelial & Stromal Dystrophies: Granular, Macular, and Lattice dystrophies (epidemiology, prevalence, specific histopathological stains like Masson Trichrome, Congo Red, Colloidal Iron)
- Endothelial Dystrophies: Fuchs' Endothelial Dystrophy, corneal guttata, and development of Bullous Keratopathy

## Ectatic Corneal Conditions (Keratoconus)
- Clinical signs (Munson's sign, Fleischer ring, Scissoring reflex, Hydrops)
- Management procedures (Corneal Collagen Cross-Linking - C3R, Intracorneal Ring Segments - INTACS, Deep Anterior Lamellar Keratoplasty - DALK)

## Metabolic & Drug-Induced Corneal Changes
- Wilson's Disease: Kayser-Fleischer (KF) ring characteristics, site of copper deposition (Descemet's membrane), and diagnostic evaluation (Serum Ceruloplasmin)
- Cornea Verticillata / Vortex Keratopathy: Drug-induced corneal whorl deposits (Amiodarone, Chloroquine, Indomethacin)

## Nutritional & Ocular Surface Deficiency States
- Vitamin A deficiency (Xerophthalmia, Bitot spots, Nyctalopia, corneal ulceration)

## Ocular Trauma & Foreign Body Evaluation
- Metallic intraocular foreign bodies: Indicated vs contraindicated imaging (Contraindication of MRI in metallic foreign body injuries)

## Keratoplasty & Eye Banking
- Corneal storage/preservation media (McCarey-Kaufman, Optisol GS)
- Surgical techniques and suture materials (10-0 Nylon)
- Congenital corneal opacities and timing of pediatric keratoplasty / management

## Refractive Surgery & Lasers
- Refractive surgical modalities (LASIK, PRK, SMILE, Radial Keratotomy) and contraindications (high-impact sports like boxing)
- Excimer laser properties (193 nm ArF excimer laser)
