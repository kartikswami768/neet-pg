# GI & Hepatobiliary Surgery — NEET-PG + INI-CET High-Yield Topics

For **NEET-PG + INI-CET**, GI and hepatobiliary surgery are much more predictable than they initially look. A relatively small set of concepts gets recycled in different forms—clinical vignette, image, investigation, management, or a “most likely” question.

I’d organize your revision around these **high-yield clusters** rather than trying to memorize the entire surgery textbook.

---

# 1. Esophagus

### Very high yield

- **Achalasia**
  - Clinical features
  - Barium swallow: bird-beak appearance
  - Manometry findings
  - Types of achalasia
  - Treatment: pneumatic dilatation vs Heller myotomy vs POEM
  - Chagas differential
- **Esophageal carcinoma**
  - Risk factors
  - Squamous cell vs adenocarcinoma
  - Common sites
  - Lymphatic drainage
  - Presentation
  - Staging
  - Treatment
- **GERD**
  - Complications
  - Barrett esophagus
  - Indications for surgery
  - Fundoplication
- **Esophageal perforation**
  - Boerhaave syndrome
  - Mallory-Weiss tear
  - Difference between them
  - Management
- **Esophageal varices**
  - Portal hypertension
  - Acute variceal bleeding management
  - Band ligation
  - Terlipressin
  - TIPS

### Typical exam questions

> A patient has dysphagia to both solids and liquids from the beginning. Diagnosis?

> Bird-beak appearance + absent peristalsis → what is the diagnosis?

> Best definitive investigation for achalasia?

> Patient develops severe chest pain and subcutaneous emphysema after forceful vomiting. Diagnosis?

> Which condition is associated with Barrett esophagus?

- DNA mismatch repair defect

**FAP**

- APC mutation

# 9. Pancreas

Another huge scoring area.

## Acute Pancreatitis

Absolutely know:

### Causes

**Gallstones + alcohol** are the big two.

Also:

- Hypertriglyceridemia
- Hypercalcemia
- Drugs
- ERCP
- Trauma
- Autoimmune

### Diagnosis

Need **2 of 3**:

- Typical abdominal pain
- Lipase/amylase elevation
- Imaging findings

### Severity

- BISAP
- Revised Atlanta classification
- Organ failure
- Necrosis

### Complications

- Acute peripancreatic fluid collection
- Pseudocyst
- Acute necrotic collection
- Walled-off necrosis
- ARDS
- AKI
- Shock

---

## Pancreatic Pseudocyst

Know:

- Definition
- Timing
- Complications
- When to intervene
- Endoscopic drainage
- Surgical drainage

And distinguish it from **walled-off pancreatic necrosis**.

---

## Chronic Pancreatitis

Very high yield.

Know:

- Alcohol
- Hereditary pancreatitis
- Tropical pancreatitis
- Obstructive causes
- Calcification
- Steatorrhea
- Diabetes
- Pain

### Surgical procedures

This is where NEET/INI likes to become annoying.

Know the differences between:

- **Puestow procedure**
- **Frey procedure**
- **Beger procedure**
- **Whipple procedure**
- **Distal pancreatectomy**

Particularly:

**Dilated pancreatic duct → drainage procedure**

**Inflammatory pancreatic head mass → Frey/Beger-type procedures**

**Tumor in head → Whipple**

- Duodenum

- Gallbladder

## Anastomoses

**Pancreas → jejunum**

# How to Study GI Surgery for NEET-PG/INI-CET

Don't study **"GI surgery" as one enormous chapter**.

Study it as:

**Esophagus → stomach → small bowel → appendix → colon → anorectal → liver → portal hypertension → gallbladder → bile ducts → pancreas → spleen**

For each organ, force yourself through the same five questions:

**1. Anatomy → 2. Common diseases → 3. Classic presentation → 4. Investigation → 5. Management**

That structure catches a surprisingly large proportion of the questions while also making the random-looking INI-CET clinical vignettes much easier to decode.
