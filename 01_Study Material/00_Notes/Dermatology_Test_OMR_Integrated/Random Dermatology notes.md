## We cut the Desmoglein by an antibody
### Pemphigus Foliaceous
- Antibodies against Desmoglein 1
- Subcornea acantholysis → Subcorneal Blister
- Subcorneal Fishnet appearance in Direct Immuno Fluoresence
- Nikolsky Sign Positive → **True Nikolsky**

### Pemphigus Vulgaris
- Antibodies against Desmoglein 3
- We will be able to see the single layer of Basal cells → that single layer is called Row of Tombstones
- Suprabasal Fishnet appearance
- Nikolsky Sign Positive → **True Nikolsky**
## We cut the Desmoglein by a toxin
### Bullous Impetigo → aka Pemphigus Neonatorum (misnomer)
- Same as Pemphigus Foliaceous (Desmoglein 1) (only the starting point is different)
- Pay attention that it has nothing to do with Pemphigus (which is antibody mediated)
- Since Pus will also be present → Pus will settle at bottom → Hypopyon Sign
- Since toxin is cause and not the antibody → **Direct immunofluorescence test → Negative**
### SSSS (Ritter's Disease → not Reiter's )
toxin – Epidermis – BI
toxin – Blood – SSSS
Positive Nikolsky Sign **True**

## Acantholytic Cells
### Pemphigus
- We don't want Circular Cells → they have gap between them → space for microbes
- Desmosome affected → Cells become circular (instead of polygonal) → Acantholytic Cells of Blister
- **Tzank Smear → Giemsa Stain → Uninucleate Acantholytic Cell**
### Herpes
- Multinucleate Acantholytic Cell
- It causes ballooning (swelling) of cells → This causes Merging of cells → Multinucleate Cells
- This way of acantholysis in which cell junctions are destroyed due to pressure and not due to problem in junction → **Secondary Acantholysis**
- Herpes have → 
	- Grouped Cells
	- Grouped Vesicles (not bulla)
