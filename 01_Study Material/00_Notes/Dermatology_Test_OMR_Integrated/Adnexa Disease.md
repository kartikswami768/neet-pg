# Acne (hair + Sebum)
- Commensal in sebaceous unit(*Cutibacterium acnes*)
- Androgen stimulation increase Sebum
	Things that increase androgen levels
	- Fat
	- Ovary (PMOS)
## Stages
### 1st lesion | Comedone (Keratin Plug)
Treatment is Keratolytic
- Adapalene
- Tretinoin
- Tazarotene
### Stage 2 Acne (papule + comedone)
- If this Stage 1 go untreated → Pressure will cause bursting of Sebaceous Gland → Bacteria will spread to nearby tissue → Inflammation → Papule
- This thing will happen to some comedones
So treatment:
1. Keratolytic like above
2. Antibiotic for treatment
	- Clinda
	- Clarithro
	- Dapsone
	- Nadifloxacin
	- **Benzoyl Peroxide** → Topical → Used in **resistance cases** and **pregnancy**
### Stage 3 
Along with all other there will be pus
- Doxy
- Mino
- Azithro (not used commonly due to resistancein Cutibacterium due to repeated exposure from sore throat)
- Lymecycline

### Stage 4
Oral Isotretinoin
→ This is Sebolytic → Small swimming pool for bacteria
→ and Keratolytic → no Comedones

**Reasons for Failure of Isotretinoin:**
1. Drug induced acne
	- acneiform eruption

	- Monomorphic (Chest, Pain)

	- Androgen will hit both Sebum glands and Hair → acne + Hirsutism
