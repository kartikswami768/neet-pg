## REspiratory Distress Syndrome
### Cause
Inadequate Surfactant: whhich causes alveoli collapse
- L/S ratio < 2 
- Lecithin is the phospholipid or Surfactant

### CXR
> **White out with air bronchogram**
> Trachea doesn't need surfactant

### Management
#### Prevention
Antenatal Steroids
- BetaMethasone 12 mg x 2 times x 24 hours apart
- Dexamethasone 6 mg x 4 times x 12 hours apart

#### Treatment
CPAP will keep airway pressure
	$\Downarrow$ Failure when (>30 % CPAP is not helping)
introduce Surfactant (InSurE)
LISA (Less Invasive Surfactant administrastion)


## Apnea of Prematurity
Prematurity < 34 week
Apnea is considered when babt doesn't breate for > 20 or less but $\downarrow$ HR/Spo2

**Cause:** Immaturity of central/peripheral chemoreceptors
**CXR: Normal**
**Treatment**:
> First rule out 2º causes
> Then **DOC: Caffeine Citrate** (inhibits adenosine

It can happen to an infant in sleep if put to sleep in prone position

## Transient Tachypnea of Newborn
### Risk Factors
- Male child
- LSCS
- Infant of Diabetic Mother

### Cause
Lack of Sympathetic stimulation of ENaC channels in the lung which causes retention of fluid in lung

### CXR
- Perihilar opacity extending into fissures of lung (Sunbirst appearance)

### Treatment: Nothing
It is self resolving in 72 hours
Only supportive care
Give oxygen if required

## Meconium Aspiration Syndrome
Happens in Post term baby (>42 weeks)

Meconium is green because Biliverdin

### Pathogenesis
1. Chemical Pneumonitis: **Patchy infiltrates** throughout the lungs
2. If Complete Obstruction: Collapse of Lung
3. Incomplete Obstruction $\rightarrow$ Ball valve $\rightarrow$ Hyperinflation $\rightarrow$ Pneumothorax

### Treatment
- Supportive Care
- Moderate to Severe Respiratory Distress
- 10% require mechanical ventilation: **HFOV (High Frequence Oscillatory Ventilation)**
- ==Currently there is **NO ROLE OF INTRATRACHEAL SUCTION OF MECONIUm**==

## Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia
M/C type: Bochdaleck
M/C site: Left Posterolateral
==Main Cause of Death: Pulmonary Hypoplasia==
**Clinical Features:** Respiratory Distress at birth + Scaphoid abdomen
**Treatment**
- Bag and mask is contraindicated
- **New Procedure**: ==EXIT (Ex Utero Intra Partum Treatment)==
	- We bring the baby out but don't cut the cord
	- Then intubate the baby and secure airway
	- Only after that we are going to cut the cord
