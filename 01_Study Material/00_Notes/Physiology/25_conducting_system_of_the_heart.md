### Modified Myocardium 00:12:32

- Modified myocardium- present in the conducting system of heart (SA node, AV node, Bundle of His, Purkinje
fibers)

### Properties Of Modified Myocardium

- Less actin and myosin proteins
- Fewer striations compared to normal myocardium
- Gap junctions are present
- Less number of cell organelles
- Every part of modified myocardium contains small round cells i.e., P-cell (Pacemaker cell)
- Can generate spontaneous impulses without help of autonomic nervous system (sympathetic and
parasympathetic system) - Automaticity property
- Also seen in a transplanted heart

### Rate Of Automaticity

SA node AV node and Bundle of His Purkinje fibres
60-80 beats/min 40-60 beats/min 15-40 beats/min
(max: 100 beats/min)
- SA node (dominant/ normal pacemaker) → inhibitory effect on other pacemaker tissue in heart (AV node,
Bundle of His and Purkinje fibers)
- This property is known as Overdrive suppression of SA node
- In normal heart, only SA node produces beats, other pacemaker cells (AV node, Bundle of His, and Purkinje
fibers) remain silent
- Silent pacemakers are known as Latent pacemakers
Complete Heart Block
- Complete heart block at the AV node → ventricle stop pumping (No impulse from SA node) → Asystole of
the ventricle
- Can persist for 20-25 sec
- If it persists for > 4 - 5 secs → Transient brain hypoxia
- Cause dizziness and fainting and fall
- Collectively known as Stokes-Adams Syndrome
- But after 20-25 sec → Purkinje fibers start generating pulse and this is known as Idioventricular rhythm
(rate 15-40 beats/minute)

### Conduction Time And Velocity

→ AV Nodal Delay
- Time required for impulse conduction from the SA node to the AV node - 30ms
- Now, Impulse travels through the AV node (5mm length)
- AV nodal delay - Total time taken required to travel through AV node - 100ms (60-125 ms, very
slow)
- Faster heart rate (faster conduction) → AV nodal delay - 60ms
- Slow heart rate → AV nodal delay - 125ms
→ HIS Bundle Delay
- Time taken to cross Bundle of His - 40ms (35-55ms)
- His bundle is located in the uppermost portion of the interventricular septum
- When an impulse crosses the Bundle of His → depolarization of ventricular muscle
- First part of ventricular muscle to depolarize - Middle part of the interventricular septum

### Impulse Conduction

- Time intervals for impulse conduction
o 30 milliseconds - to cross from SA node to the AV node
- 100 milliseconds - to cross AV node
- 40 milliseconds - to cross bundle of His
- Total time required to start ventricular depolarization for 1ª time ~ 170ms
- Total time taken to reach the apex - 200 milliseconds
- Impulse travel from apex towards the base of heart → both ventricles depolarize simultaneously
- Time taken to travel from apex to the base of the heart - 20ms
- Total time taken for complete depolarization of the heart - 220ms

### Conduction Velocity Of Impulses In Different Parts Of The Heart 00:39:45

- Slowest conduction velocity - AV node

- IT gap junctions
- Thickest fibre diameter (more diameter, less resistance)
- Action potential generated in Purkinje fiber - fast type
