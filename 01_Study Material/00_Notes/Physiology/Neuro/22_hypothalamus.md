### Reward-Punishment-Rage                                                                             00:18:46

### Reward Centre
- Medial forebrain bundle (MFB) of the hypothalamus is the primary reward center
- Stimulation of MFB induces joy and happiness

### Punishment Centre
- Periaqueductal gray matter (PAG) and periventricular area of the hypothalamus

### Rage Center
- Rage reactions result from multiple stimulations of the punishment center

### Sham Rage
- Cerebral cortex inhibits rage, maintaining emotional control
- Decerebration/ Decortication removes inhibition, leading to exaggerated rage even in response to small stimuli → Sham rage reaction
- Hypothalamus removal eliminates rage, leading to extreme placidity (excessive calmness)

### Control Of Feeding: Dual Center Model                                                        00:29:07
- Under the dual-centre model:
- Satiety center: located at the ventromedial nucleus of the hypothalamus
- Feeding center: located at the lateral nucleus of the hypothalamus
- Feeding centre
