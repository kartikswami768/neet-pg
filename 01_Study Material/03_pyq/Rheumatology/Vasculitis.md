# Classification
## Primary 
- Large Vessel
- Medium Vessel 
- Small Vessel
## Large Vessel
#### 1. Giant Cell Arteritis
- Jaw Claudication, Headache, Visual Loss
- Give Steroids before biopsy (it is an ophthalmic emergency as it can cause permanent visual loss)
- Old age (>50 yr)
#### 2. Takayasu arteritis
- Pulseless disease
- BP difference in both arms
- Aortic arch is involved
- Young female

## Medium Vessel Vasculitis
#### 1. Polyarteritis Nodosa
- Hypertension due to Renal artery involvement which causes Renal Ischemia
- Can be associated with Hepatitis B
- Spare lungs

#### 2. Kawasaki Disease
- **Strawberry tongue** (it is also a feature of scarlet fever - *Streptococcus pyogens*)
- conjunctivitis 
- Lymphadenopathy
- Coronary aneurysm
- in children

## Small Vessel Vasculitis
It is antibody associated (mostly)
**Cutaneous + Kidney + Nerves** always
### *ANCA associated* (Pauci immune)
Almost Always involve **Lungs** unlike immune complex mediated. 
Also almost always involve Kidney, nerves, skin
#### 1. GPA (Granulomatosis with polyangitis/Wegener)
- c-ANCA associated
- Fever + everything
- Everything: ENT, Lungs, Kidney, Eyes, Skin, Nerves
- characteristics: serous otitis media, Nasal septum perforation, nodular scleritis, Episcleritis, Diffuse alveolar hemmorhage
- RPGN in 60% 
*If eye is involved it’s probably GPA.*
#### 2. MPA (Microscopic Polyangitis)
p-ANCA > c-ANCA
RPGN (100%) + Lung hemorrhage
No granuloma

#### 3. Churg-Strauss / EGPA (Eosinophilic granulomatosis with polyangiitis)
- p-ANCA
Adult onset asthma with allergies  —>  eosinophilia  —>  vasculitis
- Mononeuritis multiplex

### *Immune complex mediated*
Almost always involve Kidney. Can involve Lungs. Can spare lungs 
#### 1. IgA vasculitis (Henoch-Schönlein purpura/HSP)
- can be associated with Hepatitis B or after a pulmonary infection
#### 2. [Cryoglobulinemic vasculitis](Cryoglobulinemia.md)
- usually associated with Hepatitis C
#### 3. Goodpasture syndrome

#### 4. Hypocomplementemic urticarial vasculitis (Anti C1q antibody)
