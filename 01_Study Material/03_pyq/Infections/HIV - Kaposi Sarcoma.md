## Definition
Kaposi sarcoma is a **low-grade malignant vascular tumor** arising from **endothelial cells**, strongly associated with **Human Herpesvirus-8 (HHV-8)**.

## High-Yield Associations (Very Frequently Asked)
- **HHV-8 infection** – mandatory for diagnosis  
- **HIV/AIDS** – AIDS-defining malignancy  
- Usually seen when **CD4 count < 200 cells/mm³**

## Types of Kaposi Sarcoma (PYQ Favorite)
### 1. Classic Kaposi Sarcoma
- Elderly men  
- Mediterranean / Eastern European origin  
- Slow growing  
- Mainly affects **lower limbs**

### 2. Endemic (African) Kaposi Sarcoma
- Children or young adults  
- Seen in **Sub-Saharan Africa**  
- More aggressive

### 3. Epidemic Kaposi Sarcoma
- Associated with **HIV/AIDS**  
- **Most commonly tested type**  
- Involves **skin, oral cavity, GI tract, lungs**

### 4. Iatrogenic Kaposi Sarcoma
- Post-transplant patients  
- Due to **immunosuppressive therapy**  
- May regress after reducing immunosuppression

## Clinical Features (Commonly Asked)
- **Painless violaceous (purple)** [macules → plaques → nodules](Skin%20Lesions.md)  
- Common sites:
  - Skin of face and extremities  
  - **Oral cavity – hard palate (most common oral site)**  
  - GI tract and lungs in advanced disease

## Important Differential Diagnosis (Exam Favorite)
### Kaposi Sarcoma vs Bacillary Angiomatosis
- Kaposi sarcoma: **HHV-8**, spindle cells  
- Bacillary angiomatosis: **Bartonella henselae**, neutrophils, responds to antibiotics

## Histopathology (Very High Yield)
- **Spindle-shaped endothelial cells**  
- **Slit-like vascular spaces** filled with RBCs  
- Extravasated RBCs  
- Hemosiderin-laden macrophages

## Immunohistochemistry (INI-CET Focus)
- **CD31 positive**  
- **CD34 positive**  
- **LANA-1 (HHV-8 latent nuclear antigen) positive**

## Treatment (Frequently Asked)
- **First-line in AIDS-related Kaposi sarcoma: HAART**  
- Localized disease: Radiotherapy  
- Disseminated disease: Liposomal doxorubicin

## Kaposi Sarcoma vs Angiosarcoma (Repeated PYQs)
| Feature | Kaposi Sarcoma | Angiosarcoma |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| Grade | Low grade | High grade |
| Viral association | HHV-8 present | Absent |
| Immunosuppression | Common | Not necessary |
| Aggressiveness | Less aggressive | Highly aggressive |

## Prognostic Factor (Asked in Exams)
- **Immune status (CD4 count)** is the most important prognostic factor

## One-Line Exam Pearls (Ultra High Yield)
- Purple lesions in an AIDS patient → **Kaposi sarcoma**  
- **HHV-8 is necessary but not sufficient**  
- **Hard palate involvement is classic**  
- **HAART alone can cause lesion regression**
