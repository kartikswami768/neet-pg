### Basic Concept
- **Stenosis → Pressure overload**
- **Regurgitation → Volume overload**
- **Left-sided lesions > Right-sided lesions**
- **Rheumatic heart disease most commonly affects Mitral valve first**
- **Tricuspid regurgitation is the only thing important on right side**

# Ultra-Short Rapid Revision Table

- **MS** → Diastolic rumble + Opening snap  
- **MR** → Pansystolic → Axilla  
- **AS** → Ejection systolic → Carotids + SAD  
- **AR** → Early diastolic + Wide pulse pressure  
- **MVP** → Mid-systolic click  
- **TR** → Pansystolic ↑ on inspiration
