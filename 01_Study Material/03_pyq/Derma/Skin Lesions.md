# Skin Lesions – Exam-Oriented Explanation  
(NEET PG / FMGE / INI-CET)

You are referring to **macules → plaques → nodules**, which are **types of primary skin lesions**.  
These are standard dermatology definitions and are very commonly tested in exams.

---

## PRIMARY SKIN LESIONS  
(Develop directly from the disease process)

### Macule
- Flat, **non-palpable** lesion  
- Only color change, no thickness  
- Size **< 1 cm**

**Examples (PYQ):**
- Freckles  
- Measles rash  
- Vitiligo  
- Petechiae (hemorrhagic macule)

---

### Patch
- Same as macule but **> 1 cm**

**Examples:**
- Vitiligo  
- Port-wine stain

---

### Papule
- Solid, raised, **palpable** lesion  
- Size **< 1 cm**

**Examples:**
- Lichen planus  
- Molluscum contagiosum  
- Wart (verruca)

---

### Plaque
- Flat-topped, raised lesion  
- Size **> 1 cm**  
- Often formed by coalescence of papules

**High-Yield Examples:**
- Psoriasis (classic)  
- Lichen planus  
- Leprosy plaques

---

### Nodule
- Solid, palpable lesion  
- Larger and **deeper than papule**  
- Involves dermis ± subcutis

**Examples:**
- Rheumatoid nodule  
- **Kaposi sarcoma**  
- Erythema nodosum

---

### Tumor
- Large solid mass  
- Can be benign or malignant

**Examples:**
- Lipoma  
- Basal cell carcinoma  
- Squamous cell carcinoma

---

### Vesicle
- **Fluid-filled** lesion  
- Clear fluid  
- Size **< 1 cm**

**Examples:**
- Herpes simplex  
- Herpes zoster  
- Chickenpox

---

### Bulla
- Same as vesicle but **> 1 cm**

**Examples:**
- Bullous pemphigoid  
- Pemphigus vulgaris  
- Burn blisters

---

### Pustule
- Vesicle filled with **pus**

**Examples:**
- Acne  
- Impetigo  
- Pustular psoriasis

---

### Wheal
- Transient, edematous, raised lesion  
- Disappears within hours

**Examples:**
- Urticaria  
- Insect bite reaction

---

### Cyst
- Cavity lined by epithelium  
- Contains fluid or semisolid material

**Examples:**
- Sebaceous cyst  
- Epidermoid cyst

---

## SECONDARY SKIN LESIONS  
(Result from evolution of primary lesions)

### Scale
- Flakes of stratum corneum

**Examples:**
- Psoriasis  
- Ichthyosis

---

### Crust
- Dried serum, blood, or pus

**Examples:**
- Impetigo (honey-colored crust)

---

### Erosion
- Partial loss of epidermis  
- Heals **without scar**

**Examples:**
- After rupture of vesicle

---

### Ulcer
- Loss of epidermis and dermis  
- Heals **with scar**

**Examples:**
- Venous ulcer  
- Tubercular ulcer

---

### Fissure
- Linear crack extending into dermis

**Examples:**
- Cracked heels  
- Angular cheilitis

---

### Scar
- Fibrous tissue replacing normal skin

---

### Keloid
- Excessive scar tissue  
- Extends beyond original wound

---

## VERY COMMON EXAM TRICKS

- **Macule vs Papule** → Flat vs raised  
- **Vesicle vs Bulla** → < 1 cm vs > 1 cm  
- **Erosion vs Ulcer** → No scar vs scar  
- **Plaque disease** → Think **psoriasis first**  
- **Kaposi sarcoma** → Violaceous nodules
