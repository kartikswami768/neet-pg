Vertebral artery > Posterior inferior cerebellar artery

**Structures involved**
1. Inferior cerebellar peduncle
2. Spinal lemniscus (Lateral Spinothalamic Tract)
3. CN IX, X, XI

**Symptoms**
1.  Ipsilateral
	1. bulbar paralysis (Dysphagia, dysarthria, dyspnea)
	2. loss of gag reflex
2. Contralateral
	1. loss of pain & temperature (Below face)
		- carried by Lateral Spinothalamic Tract
