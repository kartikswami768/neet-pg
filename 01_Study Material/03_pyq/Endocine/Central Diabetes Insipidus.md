- Deficient secretion of ADH from hypothalamus/pituitary
	- Inability to concentrate urine
	- Massive water loss
	- Compensatory polydipsia
## Pathophysiology
- Decreased ADH —> decreased Aquaporin 2 insertion
- It makes collecting duct impermeable to water

## Symptoms
1. [[Polyuria.canvas|Polyuria]]
	- >50 ml/kg/day or > 3 L/day
2. Nocturia
3. Polydipsia
## Investigations
  

1. Urine
	- Volume ↑↑
	- Osmolality <300 mOsm/kg (often <100)
	- Specific gravity low (<1.005)
2. Plasma
	- Osmolality ↑ or high-normal
	- Sodium ↑ if water intake inadequate
3. ADH levels
	- Low or inappropriately normal for plasma osmolality
4. **Water Deprivation Test (gold standard physiology test)**
	- Urine stays dilute
	- After desmopressin → urine osmolality ↑ significantly (>50%)
	In case of Nephrogenic DI
	- No response to desmopressin
5. **MRI Brain**
	- Absent Bright Spot
	- **Normal**: Bright spot is present due to ADH stores
	- May also show any other hypothalamic or pituitary stalk pathology

## Treatment
1. Desmopressin
2. [[Thiazide Diuretics]]
3. Low solute diet
