Different levels
1. <25 ng/ml - normal
2. 25-40 ng/ml
	- physiological rise which can occur in:
		1. Pregnancy
		2. Sleep
		3. Lactation
3. 40-100
	-  pathological rise
		1. Drugs: D2 receptor blockers
		2. Neurogenic tumor
		3. Systemic diseases
4. >100
	probably adenoma
5. >200 
	**Definitely adenoma**
