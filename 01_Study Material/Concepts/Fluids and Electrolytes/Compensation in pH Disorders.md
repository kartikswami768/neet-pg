- CO₂ is volatile (can be blown off instantly)
- Hyperventilation is physiologically easy

- Metabolic acidosis ↔ respiratory alkalosis
- Respiratory acidosis ↔ metabolic alkalosis

Correction is always double when there is body is dumping something as compared to its counterpart alkalosis.

Its easy to release $CO^{2}$ as retaining $CO^{2}$ can cause hypoxemia due to hypoventilation, similarly dumping $HCO^{3-}$ should be easier.

Changes in bicarbonate are slow as kidney work slowly as compared to lungs. So, bicarbonate levels change slowly for every $CO^{2}$ change as compared to $CO^{2}$ change for every $HCO^{3-}$ change.

Formula is always for respiratory compensation.
