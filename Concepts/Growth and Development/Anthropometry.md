## Body Proportions
### Evaluation of Short Stature:
| Age            | Upper Segment : Lower Segement |
| -------------- | ------------------------------ |
| At birth       | 1.7:1                          |
| At 3 years     | 1.3:1                          |
| At 7-10 years  | 1:1                            |
| After 10 years | 0.9:1                          |

### Evaluation of Tall Children:
**Height : Arm Span**
At birth $\rightarrow$ Height > Arm Span (Difference is 2.5 years)
At 11 years $\rightarrow$ Height = Arm Span
$\gt 11\ years \rightarrow$ Height < Arm Span (difference is *1-2 cm*)

- **Head Circumference**

| ---------------------------- | -------------------------- |

- **Chest Circumference**
	- Measured at the level of nipples
	- Midway between expiration and inspiration

| Age                | relation between HC & CC                     |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| BIRTH              | HC > CC (3 cm; 30 cm is chest circumference) |
| 6 months to 1 Year | HC = CC                                      |
| > 1 Year           | HC < CC                                      |
