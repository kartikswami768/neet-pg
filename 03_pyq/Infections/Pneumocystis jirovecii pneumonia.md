

Pneumocystis jirovecii (formerly *Pneumocystis carinii*) is an **opportunistic fungal infection** causing pneumonia, classically in **immunocompromised patients**. This organism is a **frequent favorite in NEET PG, FMGE, and INI-CET** due to its unique features and exam traps.

---

## High-Yield Basics (Very Commonly Asked)

- **Organism type:** Fungus (NOT protozoa)
- **Cell membrane:** Lacks **ergosterol**
- **Culture:** Cannot be cultured on routine media
- **Disease caused:** Pneumocystis pneumonia (PCP)
- **Common population:** HIV/AIDS and other immunosuppressed states

---

## Risk Factors (Repeated Exam Questions)

### Most important:
- HIV with **CD4 count < 200 cells/µL**

### Others:
- Organ transplant recipients  
- Long-term corticosteroid therapy  
- Malignancy  
- Congenital immunodeficiency  

---

## Clinical Features (High-Yield Presentation)

Typical symptoms:
- Progressive **dyspnea**
- **Dry cough** (non-productive)
- Fever
- **Severe hypoxia out of proportion to chest X-ray findings**

### Exam Pearl:
- HIV patient + severe hypoxia + minimal chest findings → **Think Pneumocystis**

---

## Radiology (Frequently Tested)

### Chest X-ray:
- **Bilateral diffuse interstitial infiltrates**
- Ground-glass appearance

### HRCT:
- **Ground-glass opacities** (more sensitive)

---

## Diagnosis (VERY High Yield)

- Cannot be cultured → diagnosis by **demonstration of organism**

### Specimens:
- Induced sputum  
- **Bronchoalveolar lavage (BAL)** – most sensitive

### Stains (Classic Exam Favorite):
- **Gomori Methenamine Silver (GMS)** – stains cyst wall (most common answer)
- Toluidine blue  
- Calcofluor white  

### Morphology:
- Cup-shaped cysts  
- Foamy, honeycomb appearance filling alveoli  

---

## Treatment (Must Remember Exactly)

### Drug of Choice:
- **Trimethoprim–Sulfamethoxazole (TMP-SMX)**

### If Sulfa Allergy:
- Pentamidine (IV)
- Atovaquone (oral, mild cases)

### Adjunct Therapy (Very Frequently Asked):
- **Corticosteroids** if:
  - PaO₂ < 70 mmHg  
  - A–a gradient > 35 mmHg  

---

## Prophylaxis (Extremely High Yield)

### Indication:
- HIV patients with **CD4 < 200 cells/µL**

### Drug of Choice:
- **TMP-SMX**

### Alternatives:
- Dapsone  
- Atovaquone  
- Aerosolized pentamidine  

---

## Common Exam Traps (Very Important)

- Pneumocystis is a **fungus**, but **does NOT respond to amphotericin B**
- Absence of ergosterol explains resistance to many antifungals
- **Dry cough** helps differentiate from bacterial pneumonia
- HIV + ground-glass opacity → **Pneumocystis until proven otherwise**

---

## One-Line Exam Favorites

- AIDS patient + CD4 < 200 + dry cough + hypoxia → **Pneumocystis jirovecii**
- Best stain → **Gomori methenamine silver**
- Drug of choice → **TMP-SMX**
- Prophylaxis indication → **CD4 < 200**
