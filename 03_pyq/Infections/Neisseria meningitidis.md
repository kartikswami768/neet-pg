Neisseria meningitis has a lot of important virulence factors and is very virulent. But in 5% individuals it exist as a normal flora and makes them carriers. Now carriers have antibodies against them but mostly for the specific strain. So, in large community gatherings these carriers can cause disease in even other carriers.

High-risk groups are:
1) Infants aged 6 months to 2 years
2) Army recruits
3) College freshmen
## Prophylaxis
1. **Mass chemoprophylaxis: Ciprofloxacin** 
	- but remember that ciprofloxacin is contraindicated in pregnant women and children (below 18 years) and children are one of the major portion of susceptible individuals
	- Other options for chemoprophylaxis: Rifampicin (Was DOC earlier), ceftriaxone
2. Pregnant - Ceftriaxone 
3. Child - Rifampicin
4. Neonate - Specialist decision
