## Virus Basics
- DNA virus (partially double-stranded)
- Family: **Hepadnaviridae**
- Dane particle = complete virion
- Envelope contains **HBsAg**
- Nucleocapsid contains **HBcAg + HBeAg**
- Polymerase functions:
	- DNA-dependent DNA polymerase
	- RNA-dependent DNA polymerase
	- RNase H

---

## Serological Markers (MOST TESTED)

### 1. HBsAg
- First marker to appear
- Marker of **active infection**
- Persistence >6 months → **chronic HBV**

### 2. Anti-HBs
- Marker of **recovery or vaccination**
- Only marker present after vaccination

### 3. HBcAg
- **Never detected in serum**
- Only inside hepatocytes

### 4. Anti-HBc IgM
- Marker of **acute infection**
- Marker of **window period**

### 5. Anti-HBc IgG
- Marker of **past infection**
- Present in **chronic hepatitis**

### 6. HBeAg
- Marker of **viral replication & infectivity**

### 7. Anti-HBe
- Indicates **seroconversion**
- Lower infectivity

---

## Window Period (FAVORITE CONCEPT)
- HBsAg: negative
- Anti-HBs: negative
- **ONLY Anti-HBc IgM positive**

---

## Acute Hepatitis B
- Usually self-limiting
- Labs:
  - ↑ ALT (more than AST)
- Treatment:
  - **Supportive only**
- Antivirals NOT routinely indicated

---

## Chronic Hepatitis B (VERY HIGH YIELD)

### Definition
- HBsAg positive > **6 months**

### Phases (ASKED INDIRECTLY)

#### Immune-tolerant phase
- HBeAg positive
- High HBV DNA
- Normal ALT
- No treatment

#### Immune-active phase
- HBeAg positive or negative
- ↑ ALT
- ↑ HBV DNA
- **Treatment indicated**

#### Inactive carrier state
- HBsAg positive
- Anti-HBe positive
- Normal ALT
- Low HBV DNA

---

## HBeAg-Negative Chronic Hepatitis
- Due to **precore/core promoter mutation**
- HBeAg negative but HBV DNA high
- Still active disease
- **Commonly asked trap**

---

## Indications for Treatment (NEET-PG CORE)
Treat if:
- HBV DNA ↑ AND
- ALT ↑ AND/OR
- Evidence of fibrosis/cirrhosis

---

## Drugs Used (REPEATEDLY ASKED)

### First-line
- **Tenofovir**
- **Entecavir**

### NOT preferred now
- Lamivudine (high resistance)

---

## Duration of Treatment
- **Long-term / often lifelong**
- Especially if:
  - Cirrhosis
  - HBeAg-negative disease

---

## Hepatitis B in Pregnancy (HIGH-YIELD)
- If HBV DNA high:
	- **Tenofovir in 3rd trimester**
- Newborn:
	- **HBIG + HBV vaccine at birth**

---

## Vaccination (VERY COMMON)
- Recombinant vaccine (HBsAg)
- Schedule:
	- 0, 1, 6 months
- Protective Anti-HBs ≥10 IU/L

---

## Post-Exposure Prophylaxis
- Unvaccinated:
	- HBIG + vaccine
- Vaccinated with low Anti-HBs:
	- Booster ± HBIG

---

## Complications (ASKED AS ASSOCIATIONS)
- Cirrhosis
- Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)
- IgA Vasculitis
- Polyarteritis nodosa
- Membranous nephropathy

---

## Key NEET PG One-Liners
- Only marker in window period → **Anti-HBc IgM**
- Vaccinated patient marker → **Anti-HBs only**
- Chronic HBV definition → **HBsAg >6 months**
- HBeAg-negative chronic hepatitis → **precore mutation**
- DOC chronic HBV → **Tenofovir / Entecavir**
