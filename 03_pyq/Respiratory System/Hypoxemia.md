# Causes of Hypoxemia

Hypoxemia = ↓ PaO₂ (arterial oxygen).  
Clinically, there are **5 core mechanisms**.

---

## 1. ↓ Inspired Oxygen (Low FiO₂)
**Problem:** Not enough oxygen coming in

**Examples:**
- High altitude
- Suffocation, enclosed space

**Key points:**
- ↓ PaO₂, ↓ PaCO₂ (hyperventilation)
- **A–a gradient = normal**

---

## 2. Hypoventilation
**Problem:** Not enough air reaching alveoli

**Causes:**
- CNS depression (opioids, anesthesia)
- Neuromuscular: myasthenia gravis, GBS
- Chest wall disorders: kyphoscoliosis
- Severe obesity (obesity hypoventilation)

**ABG pattern:**
- ↓ PaO₂, ↑ PaCO₂  
- **A–a gradient = normal**

> Think: *ventilation problem, not gas exchange problem*

---

## 3. Diffusion Defect
**Problem:** Oxygen cannot cross alveolar membrane properly

**Causes:**
- Interstitial lung disease (fibrosis)
- Pulmonary edema
- ARDS

**Key features:**
- Worse on exertion
- Improves with O₂

**ABG:**
- ↓ PaO₂  
- **A–a gradient = increased**

---

## 4. Ventilation–Perfusion (V/Q) Mismatch ⭐ (Most common)
**Problem:** Air and blood are not matching

### Types:
- **Low V/Q (shunt-like)** → perfusion > ventilation  
  - COPD  
  - Asthma  
  - Pneumonia  

- **High V/Q (dead space)** → ventilation > perfusion  
  - Pulmonary embolism  

**ABG:**
- ↓ PaO₂  
- **A–a gradient = increased**

**Response to O₂:**
- Improves

> Most common cause clinically

---

## 5. Right-to-Left Shunt ❗
**Problem:** Blood bypasses lungs → no oxygenation

**Types:**
- **Anatomic:**  
  - Congenital heart disease (e.g., TOF)
- **Physiologic:**  
  - Severe pneumonia  
  - ARDS  
  - Atelectasis  

**Key feature:**
- **Does NOT improve with oxygen**

**ABG:**
- ↓ PaO₂  
- **A–a gradient = increased**

## Quick Comparison Table

| Cause | A–a Gradient | PaCO₂ | O₂ Response |
|------|-------------|------|-------------|
| Low FiO₂ | Normal | ↓ | Improves |
| Hypoventilation | Normal | ↑ | Improves |
| Diffusion defect | ↑ | Normal/↓ | Improves |
| V/Q mismatch | ↑ | Normal/↓ | Improves |
| Shunt | ↑ | Normal | ❌ No improvement |

## High-yield Clinical Pearls

- **Normal A–a gradient → think:**  
  - Hypoventilation  
  - High altitude  

- **Increased A–a gradient → think lung pathology**

- **O₂ not helping = SHUNT until proven otherwise**

- **Exercise worsens diffusion defect** (classic viva point)
