
### Causes
- Rheumatic heart disease  
- Mitral valve prolapse  
- Ischemic heart disease (papillary muscle dysfunction)

### Pathology
Systolic backflow into LA → LA + LV volume overload → Dilatation  

### Clinical Features
- Dyspnea  
- Fatigue  
- Atrial fibrillation  

### Auscultation
- Pansystolic (holosystolic) murmur  
- Best heard at apex  
- Radiates to axilla  

**Buzzword:** Pansystolic murmur → Axilla  

**Important:** Acute MR (post-MI papillary muscle rupture) → Pulmonary edema
