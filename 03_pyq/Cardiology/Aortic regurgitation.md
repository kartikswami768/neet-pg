### Causes
- Rheumatic heart disease  
- Infective endocarditis  
- Marfan syndrome  
- Aortic dissection  

### Pathology
Diastolic backflow into LV → LV volume overload → Eccentric hypertrophy  

### Key Signs
- Wide pulse pressure  
- Bounding pulse (Water hammer pulse)  
- Head bobbing (de Musset sign)  
- Quincke sign (capillary pulsation)

### Auscultation
- Early diastolic decrescendo murmur  
- Left sternal border  

**Buzzword:** Wide pulse pressure + Early diastolic murmur
