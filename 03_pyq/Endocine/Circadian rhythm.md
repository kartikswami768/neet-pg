# Suprachiasmatic Nucleus
**Location:** Anterior hypothalamus, just above chiasma

**Function:** MASTER BIOLOGICAL CLOCK
**Mechanism:** Light —> Retina —> retinohypothalamic tract —> SCN (direct light input)

# Hormone regulation
## 1. Melatonin
Synchronises circadian rhythm
Promotes sleep
- Light suppresses melatonin

## 2. Cortisol
- Peaks early morning (~6 - 8 am)
- Lowest midnight

Promotes wakefulness, alertness

## 3. Growth hormone, Prolactin
Peaks during deep sleep

## 4. Body Temperature Rhythm
Lowest early morning
Highest evening
