## Manifestations
### Articular Features
1. Symmetric polyarthritis
2. Small joints
	- MCP
	- PIP
	- Wrist
3. Spare DIP (which is involved in Osteoarthrits)
#### Cardinal Symptom
Morning stiffness that persist for more than 1 hour

#### Deformities 
Ulnar deviation
Swan-neck deformity
Z-thumb 
### Extra articular manifestations
1. Subcutaneous nodules
2. Lung
	- Interstitial lung disease
	- Pleural effusion
3. Heart
	- Pericarditis
	- Acceleerated atheroslerosis
4. Eye
	-  Episcleritis
	- Scleritis
5. Anemia
