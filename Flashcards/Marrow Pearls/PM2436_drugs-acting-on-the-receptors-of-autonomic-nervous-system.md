# Drugs acting on the receptors of autonomic nervous system

Pearl ID: PM2436

?

### Cholinergic drugs
![[images/PM2436_cholinergic_drugs.png]]

- **Acetylcholinesterase inhibitors / anticholinesterases:** Edrophonium, Neostigmine, Pyridostigmine.
- **Cholinergic agonists**
  - Non-selective: Acetylcholine, Carbachol, Pilocarpine
  - Muscarinic: Methacholine, Bethanechol
  - Nicotinic: Nicotine, Epibatidine, Cytisine

### Anticholinergic drugs
![[images/PM2436_anticholinergic_drugs.png]]

- **Muscarinic receptor antagonist:** Atropine
- **Nicotinic receptor antagonists**
  - Nm: d-Tubocurarine, Atracurium
  - Nn: Trimethaphan

### Adrenergic drugs — receptor agonists
![[images/PM2436_adrenergic_agonists.png]]

- **α receptor agonists**
  - Non-selective: Oxymetazoline
  - α1-selective: Phenylephrine
  - α2 receptor action: Clonidine
- **β receptor agonists**
  - Non-selective: Isoproterenol
  - β1 + α1 action: Dobutamine
  - β2 selective: Terbutaline, Albuterol, Metaproterenol

### Adrenergic receptor antagonists
![[images/PM2436_adrenergic_antagonists.png]]

- **α receptor antagonists**
  - Nonselective: Phenoxybenzamine, Phentolamine
  - α1-selective: Prazosin, Terazosin, Doxazosin, Alfuzosin, Tamsulosin, Indoramin, Urapidil, Bunazosin
  - α2-selective: Yohimbine
- **β receptor antagonists**
  - Nonselective (first generation): Nadolol, Penbutolol, Pindolol, Propranolol, Timolol, Sotalol, Levobunolol, Metipranolol
  - β1-selective (second generation): Acebutolol, Atenolol, Bisoprolol, Esmolol, Metoprolol
  - Nonselective (third generation): Carteolol, Carvedilol, Bucindolol, Labetalol
  - β1-selective (third generation): Betaxolol, Celiprolol, Nebivolol
