**What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Comparison of Mutational and Transferable Drug Resistance" (PM0452)?**

?

| Mutational drug resistance | Transferable drug resistance |
|---|---|
| One drug resistance at a time | Multiple drug resistance |
| Low degree resistance | High degree resistance |
| Can be overcome by high drug dose | High dose ineffective |
| Drug combinations can prevent resistance | Drug combinations cannot prevent resistance |
| Resistance does not spread | Spreads to same or different species |
| Mutants may be defective | Not defective |
| Virulence may be low | Virulence not decreased |
