### Classification

| Site/action | Drugs |
|---|---|
| Inhibit iodide trapping | Thiocyanates, perchlorates, nitrates |
| Inhibit hormone synthesis | Propylthiouracil, methimazole, carbimazole |
| Inhibit hormone release | Iodine, sodium iodide, potassium iodide, organic iodide |
| Inhibit peripheral conversion | Propylthiouracil, amiodarone, iodinated contrast dyes (iopanoic acid), propranolol, glucocorticoids |
| Destroy thyroid tissue | Radioactive iodine |

### Key diagram labels

- **Trapping:** thiocyanates, perchlorates, nitrates
- **Thyroid peroxidase pathway:** propylthiouracil, carbimazole, methimazole
- **Proteolysis / secretion:** iodides
- **Peripheral deiodination:** propylthiouracil
- **Thyroid tissue destruction:** radioactive iodine
- **MIT:** monoiodotyrosine
- **DIT:** diiodotyrosine
- **TG:** thyroglobulin
