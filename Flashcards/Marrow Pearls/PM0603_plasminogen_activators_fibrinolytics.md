### Fibrin non-specific plasminogen activators

- **Streptokinase** - least fibrin specific and has the lowest incidence of intracranial hemorrhage (as stated in the pearl)
- **Anistreplase**
- **Urokinase**

### Fibrin-specific plasminogen activators

- **Alteplase** - shortest half-life
- **Tenecteplase** - most fibrin specific and given as a single bolus dose
- **Reteplase**
