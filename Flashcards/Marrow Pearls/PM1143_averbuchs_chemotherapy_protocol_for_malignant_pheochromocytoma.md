# Averbuch's Chemotherapy Protocol for Malignant Pheochromocytoma

Pearl ID: PM1143

#flashcards

**What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Averbuch's Chemotherapy Protocol for Malignant Pheochromocytoma" (PM1143)?**

?
Averbuch's chemotherapy protocol for malignant pheochromocytoma includes:
- Dacarbazine 600 mg/m² on days 1 and 2
- Cyclophosphamide 750 mg/m² on day 1
- Vincristine 1.4 mg/m² on day 1

All are repeated every 21 days for three to six cycles.
