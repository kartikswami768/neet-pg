  # Treatment Ladder for Psoriasis

  Pearl ID: PM0296

  #review

  **What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Treatment Ladder for Psoriasis" (PM0296)?**

  ?

  ### I. Mild psoriasis without arthritis

  **First line**
  - Coal tar
  - Dithranol
  - Potent topical steroid
  - Vitamin D analogue

  **Second line**
  - Local NB-UVB or PUVA
  - Excimer laser

  ### II. Moderate or severe psoriasis without arthritis

  **First line**
  - NB-UVB or PUVA

  **Second line**
  - Acitretin
  - Apremilast
  - Ciclosporin
  - Fumaric acid esters (where available)
  - Methotrexate

  **Third line**
  - Adalimumab
  - Etanercept
  - Infliximab
  - Secukinumab
  - Ustekinumab

  ### III. Moderate or severe psoriasis with psoriatic arthritis

  **First line**
  - Apremilast
  - Methotrexate

  **Second line**
  - Adalimumab
  - Etanercept
  - Infliximab
  - Ustekinumab

  **Third line**
  - Combination therapy
