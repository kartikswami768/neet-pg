**What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Physiologic effects of thyroid hormone" (PM1490)?**

?
| System | Effect |
|---|---|
| Cardiovascular | Chronotropic and inotropic actions; ↑ number of β-adrenergic receptors; ↑ response to circulating catecholamines; ↑ proportion of α-myosin heavy chain; ↓ peripheral vascular resistance; ↑ blood volume (activates RAAS) |
| Musculoskeletal | Catabolic action (protein breakdown); promotes normal growth and skeletal maturation |
| Nervous | Promotes normal brain development |
| Gastrointestinal | ↑ appetite and food intake; ↑ GI motility |
| Respiratory | ↑ respiratory rate |
| Others | Carbohydrates: promotes uptake of glucose into cells by ↑ insulin secretion, ↑ gluconeogenesis and ↑ glycogenolysis; lipids: catabolic action (stimulates lipolysis) and ↓ LDL (formation of LDL receptors); calorigenic: ↑ basal metabolic rate; thermogenic: heat production |
