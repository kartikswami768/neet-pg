### Naloxone
Short-acting opioid antagonist with duration of action of 1-2 hours. Used in opioid overdose and respiratory depression in newborns due to perinatal opioid use.

### Naltrexone
Long-acting opioid antagonist with effects lasting till 48 hours. Used for prevention of relapse in opioid addiction treatment. It is associated with hepatotoxicity. It does not suppress craving in opioid addiction but suppresses craving in chronic alcoholics.

### Nalmefene
Long-acting opioid antagonist with a half-life of 8-10 hours; administered intravenously. Used in opioid overdose like naloxone.
