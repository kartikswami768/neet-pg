| Feature | Type 1 Reaction (Reversal/Upgrading) | Type 2 Reaction (Erythema Nodosum Leprosum) |
|---|---|---|
| Usual spectrum | Mainly borderline spectrum (BT, BB, BL) | Mainly LL (50%) > BL (15%) |
