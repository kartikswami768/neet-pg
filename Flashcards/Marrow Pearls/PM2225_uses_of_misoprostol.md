### Uses and primary doses of misoprostol

| Indication | Primary dose of misoprostol | Remarks |
|---|---|---|
| Medical termination of pregnancy — combined regimen, up to 12 weeks | 800 mcg vaginally, buccally or sublingually | Ideally used 1–2 days after 200 mg mifepristone in the combined regimen |
| Medical termination of pregnancy — combined regimen, beyond 12 weeks | 400 mcg vaginally, buccally or sublingually every 3 hours | Mucosal route preferred over oral route |
| Medical termination — misoprostol-only, up to 12 weeks | 800 mcg vaginally, buccally or sublingually | Doses also apply for incomplete and missed abortion |
| Medical termination — misoprostol-only, beyond 12 weeks | 400 mcg vaginally, buccally or sublingually every 3 hours |  |
| Surgical abortion — cervical preparation | 400 mcg vaginally/sublingually | Another method: oral mifepristone 200 mg 24–48 h before the procedure |
| PPH prophylaxis | 800 mcg sublingual single dose OR 600 mcg oral/sublingual/rectal single dose for PPH secondary prevention (approximately >350 mL blood loss) | Where oxytocin is not available or storage conditions are inadequate |
| PPH treatment | 800 mcg sublingual single dose |  |

### Timing of abortion methods in the source
- 12–14 weeks: both medical and surgical methods can be done
- >14 weeks: surgical method
