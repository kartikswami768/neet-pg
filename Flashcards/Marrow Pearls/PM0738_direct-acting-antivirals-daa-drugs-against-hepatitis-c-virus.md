### Classes
| Mechanism of action | Drug examples in the source Pearl |
|---|---|
| **NS5A inhibitors (-asvir)** | Velpatasvir, Daclatasvir, Ledipasvir, Ombitasvir, Elbasvir |
| **NS5B nucleos(t)ide polymerase inhibitor (-buvir)** | Sofosbuvir |
| **NS5B non-nucleos(t)ide polymerase inhibitor** | Dasabuvir, Beclabuvir |
| **NS3/4A inhibitors (-previr)** | Boceprevir, Telaprevir, Simeprevir, Paritaprevir, Asunaprevir, Grazoprevir |

### Regimens listed in the source
- Acute, chronic hepatitis, or compensated cirrhosis: the source lists **Velpatasvir**.
- Decompensated cirrhosis: the source lists **Daclatasvir** and other NS5A agents.
- **Velpatasvir (NS5A inhibitor) + Sofosbuvir (NS5B inhibitor) × 12 weeks.**
- **Velpatasvir + Sofosbuvir + Ribavirin × 12 weeks**, with ribavirin described as a synthetic nucleotide analogue.
