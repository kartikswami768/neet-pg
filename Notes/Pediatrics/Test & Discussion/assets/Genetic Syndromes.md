## Turner Syndrome
- 60% cases: XO 
- Rest: Partial Monosomy or Mosaic

### Clinical Features
1. Short Stature 
> 	Mutation in SHOX gene
2. 3W:
	1. Webbed Neck
	2. Wide Carrying angle
	3. Widely separated nipple
3. Gonads: 
	1. Streak Ovaries
	2. No menses/fertility
4. CVS
	1. Bicuspid aortic valve > coarction of aorta
5. Lymphatic System:
	1. Cystic Hygroma
6. Horseshoe Kidney
7. **IQ is NORMAL**
8. Short 4th Metacarpal
	1. Dimple will be there instead of knucle
	2. KKDK
9. Barr Bodies absent

## Klinefelter Syndrome
- Due to XXY
- No of Barr Bodies: 1
- Since Sex aneuploidy: **Infertility**
### Clinical Features
> Males who have gone feminisation due to extra X
- Tall Stature --> `Also a feature of Marfan Syndrome`
- No facial hair
- Thin Limbs
- Breast +
- IQ is low --> Mental Retardation is directly proportional to no of x chromosome
- Increased risk of Gonadoblastoma
- Gonads --> ==Hyalanisation fo Seminiferous Tubules== 

## Marfan Syndrome
### Clinical Features
- Steinberg sign positive: thumb will reach outside other end of palm on folding of thumb
- FIBRILLIN 15 mutaion positive
- Ectopia lentis
- Mitral Valve prolapse
- Aortic Regurgitation

# Imprinting Disorders
> [!info] What are they?
> It is basically inactivation of one gene that body has decided randomly which one of the two allelles it is going to inactivate
> 
> **Imprinting Disorder**
> - Deletion of active paternal/maternal gene
> - While other gene is abnorma
> - In 10% cases body will make copy of the available gene allelle but that will only cause **disomy of inactive gene**
