Nephritic Syndrome: Glomerulonephritis
**Clinical Features:**
- ==Hallmark: Hematuria==
1. Protein + (1/2+ on dipstick) + Increased BP
	1. Vasodilation in glomerulus 
	2. This causes intraglomerular hypotension
	3. This triggers RAAS
2. RBC casts
3. Dysmorphic RBC

Most Common; 
1. PSGN: 
	1. immune complex deposition
	2. Cola coloured urine

# Nephrotic
Definition:
1. Nephrotic range proteinuria: 
	1. Dipstick: 3/4+
	2. Up/Uc > 2 (normally it is < 2)
	3. > 40 mg/m$^2$ per hour
2. Hypoalbuminemia (<3 g/dL)
3. Generalised Edema

Hypercholesterolemia can also be seen
