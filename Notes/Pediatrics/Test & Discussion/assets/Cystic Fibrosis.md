
Mutation: CFTR
	Mc ∆F508 $\rightarrow$ Deletion of phenylalanine
Inheritence: AR
Chromosome: 7

## Pathogenesis
![[Cystic Fibrosis 2026-09-19 19.09.31.excalidraw]]
![[Cystic Fibrosis 2026-09-19 19.09.31.excalidraw]]

1. Recurrent LRTI: Bronchiectasis
	1. Mc Staph aureus > Pseudomonas
2. Pancreas
	1. Exocrine insufficiency
		1. Fat malabsorption
		2. Deficiecny of vitamin a, d, e, k
	2. Recurrent episodes of Pancreatitis: 
		1. T1DM
3. Gut
	1. Newborn: Delated Meconium Passage, Neonatal Cholestasis
	2. Rectal Prolapse
	3. DIOS (Distal Intestinal Obstruction Syndrome)
4. Nasal polyp
5. Congenital obstruction of vas defererens $\rightarrow$ Obstructive azoospermia

**IN Sweat Glands**: CFTR tries to preserve NaCl in sweat
But since it is defective here also increased loss of Na and Cl in sweat
So they will have HypoKalemia and metabolic acidosis through RAAS

## Diagnostic Criteria
1. Signs & SYmptoms of CD OR
2. Family history of Cystic FIbrosis OR
3. Positive Newborn Screening (immunoreactive Trypsinogen)
4. Plus any one of `incomplete`
## Treatment
1. Chest physiotherapy
2. Pancreateic Enzyme replacement
3. New Drugs
	1. LUME-CAFTOR
	2. IVA-CAFTOR (Cl channel potentiator)
