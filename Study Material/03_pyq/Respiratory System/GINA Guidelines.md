# Diagnosis
It requires both of the following:
- **Typical variable respiratory symptoms**
- **Evidence of variable airflow limitation**
### Variable respiratory Symptoms
- Wheeze
- Shortness of breath
- Chest tightness
- Cough
### Variable airflow limitation
- FEV1 increases >= 12% and >= 200ml after bronchodilator
- Peak flow is variable
- Positive Bronchial Challenge test
- Significant improvement after ICS treatment

# GINA 2025 Track 1 (Preferred) – Adults & Adolescents

## Definitions

### AIR (Anti-Inflammatory Reliever)
- ICS-formoterol used only when symptoms occur.

### MART (Maintenance And Reliever Therapy)
- Daily ICS-formoterol maintenance.
- The same ICS-formoterol inhaler is also used as the reliever.

| Step       | Preferred Track 1 Treatment                                                               | Typical Patient                                                |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Step 1** | As-needed low-dose ICS-formoterol (**AIR**)                                               | Symptoms less than about twice/month and no major risk factors |
| **Step 2** | As-needed low-dose ICS-formoterol (**AIR**)                                               | Symptoms more frequent than Step 1 but still mild asthma       |
| **Step 3** | Low-dose ICS-formoterol **MART**                                                          | Asthma uncontrolled on Step 2                                  |
| **Step 4** | Medium-dose ICS-formoterol **MART**                                                       | Asthma uncontrolled on Step 3                                  |
| **Step 5** | Refer for specialist assessment; consider high-dose ICS-formoterol, LAMA, biologics, etc. | Severe asthma despite Step 4 treatment                         |
