# AIDS-Defining Illnesses

## Definition
A person with **HIV infection** is diagnosed with **AIDS** if **any one** of the following is present:
- **Any AIDS-defining illness**, **OR**
- **CD4 count < 200 cells/µL**

---

## Opportunistic Infections

- **[[Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia]] (PCP)** – most common
- **Tuberculosis** (pulmonary or extrapulmonary)
- **Disseminated Mycobacterium avium complex (MAC)**
- **Candidiasis of esophagus, trachea, bronchi, or lungs**
- **Cryptococcal meningitis**
- **Toxoplasma gondii encephalitis**
- **Cytomegalovirus (CMV) retinitis** or other end-organ disease
- **Chronic cryptosporidiosis** (> 1 month diarrhea)
- **Chronic isosporiasis**
- **Recurrent bacterial pneumonia**
- **Progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (JC virus)**
- **Recurrent Salmonella septicemia**

---

## Malignancies

- **[[HIV - Kaposi Sarcoma]]**
- **Non-Hodgkin lymphoma**  
  - Especially **primary CNS lymphoma**
- **Invasive carcinoma of cervix**

---

## Other AIDS-Defining Conditions

- **HIV encephalopathy (AIDS dementia complex)**
- **Wasting syndrome**

---

## Important Exam Notes

- **Oral candidiasis** → *NOT* AIDS
- **Herpes zoster** → *NOT* AIDS (unless severe/recurrent)
- **Persistent generalized lymphadenopathy** → *NOT* AIDS
- **Pulmonary tuberculosis** → **AIDS-defining**
