## Parkinson Plus Syndromes (NEET PG / FMGE High-Yield)

### Definition  
Parkinson Plus syndromes are neurodegenerative disorders characterized by **parkinsonism with additional features** and **poor or absent response to Levodopa**.

>Keep focus on NEET one liners.

**Core Exam Clue**  
Poor Levodopa response + early autonomic, cognitive, or postural symptoms → **Parkinson Plus**

---

## Commonly Asked Types

### 1. Progressive Supranuclear Palsy (PSP)  
**Most common Parkinson Plus syndrome**

**Key Features**
- Early postural instability and falls  
- **Supranuclear gaze palsy**  
- Vertical gaze affected first (especially downgaze)  
- Axial rigidity > limb rigidity  
- Pseudobulbar palsy  
- Dysarthria, dysphagia  

**Imaging Sign**
- **Hummingbird sign / Penguin sign** (MRI)
	- atrophy of midbrain with preservation of pons

![[IMG_0905.jpeg]]

**NEET PG One-liner**  
Early falls + vertical gaze palsy = **PSP**

---

### 2. Multiple System Atrophy (MSA)

**Types**
- MSA-P (Parkinsonian type)  
- MSA-C (Cerebellar type)

**Key Features**
- Prominent **autonomic failure**
- Orthostatic hypotension  
- Urinary incontinence  
- Erectile dysfunction  
- Cerebellar ataxia (MSA-C)  
- Poor Levodopa response  

**Imaging Signs**
- **Hot cross bun sign** (pons)  
![[IMG_0906.jpeg]]
![[IMG_0907.png]]
- Putaminal rim sign  

**NEET PG One-liner**  
Parkinsonism + severe autonomic dysfunction = **MSA**

---

### 3. Corticobasal Degeneration (CBD)

**Key Features**
- Asymmetric parkinsonism  
- **Apraxia**  
- **Alien limb phenomenon**  
>Alien limb phenomenon = involuntary but purposeful limb movements with loss of sense of ownership, classically seen in corticobasal degeneration or corpus callosum lesions.
- Cortical sensory loss  
- Myoclonus  

**NEET PG One-liner**  
Alien limb + asymmetric rigidity = **CBD**

---

### 4. Dementia with Lewy Bodies (DLB)
Lewy Bodies spread beyond substantia nigra (from early stage).
	It is different form **Parkinson’s disease dementia** as in PDD, Lewy bodies spread to cortex in **later stages**

**Key Features**
- **Early dementia** (before or within 1 year of parkinsonism)  
- Visual hallucinations  
- Fluctuating cognition  
- REM sleep behavior disorder  

**NEET PG One-liner**  
Early dementia + visual hallucinations = **DLB**

---

## Parkinson Disease vs Parkinson Plus (Very High-Yield)

### Parkinson Disease
- Good Levodopa response  
- Rest tremor predominant  
- Late falls  
- No early autonomic failure  

### Parkinson Plus
- Poor Levodopa response  
- Early falls  
- Early autonomic dysfunction or dementia  
- Additional signs (gaze palsy, ataxia, apraxia)

---

## Rapid One-Liner Recall Table

- **PSP** → Vertical gaze palsy, early falls  
- **MSA** → Autonomic failure, hot cross bun sign  
- **CBD** → Alien limb, asymmetric rigidity  
- **DLB** → Early dementia, visual hallucinations
