
Subacute combined degeneration is a **neurological disorder caused by vitamin B12 deficiency**, leading to degeneration of specific parts of the spinal cord and peripheral nerves. 

---

## Meaning of the Term
- **Subacute**: Symptoms develop gradually over weeks to months  
- **Combined**: Multiple spinal cord tracts are involved

## Cause
- Most commonly due to **vitamin B12 (cobalamin) deficiency**

## Etiology
- Pernicious anemia (autoimmune destruction of intrinsic factor)
- Post-gastrectomy
- Malabsorption (Crohn disease, celiac disease)
- Strict vegan diet
- Chronic nitrous oxide exposure
- Long-term metformin use
## Pathophysiology
Vitamin B12 is essential for myelin synthesis. Deficiency causes defective myelination and demyelination of:
- **Posterior columns** → Loss of vibration sense and proprioception  
- **Lateral corticospinal tracts** → Upper motor neuron signs  
- **Peripheral nerves** → Peripheral neuropathy
- Spinocerebellar tracts (occasionally)

This leads to the term **“combined” degeneration**.

## Clinical Features

### Sensory
- Paresthesias (tingling, numbness)
- Loss of vibration and position sense
- Sensory ataxia
- Positive Romberg sign

### Motor
- Spasticity
- Weakness
- Hyperreflexia
- Babinski sign (extensor plantar response)

### Gait
- Ataxic, stamping gait

### Neuropsychiatric
- Memory impairment
- Depression
- Irritability
- Dementia (severe cases)

## Associated Systemic Features
- Megaloblastic anemia
- Glossitis (beefy red tongue)
- Pallor
- Fatigue

> **Important Exam Point**: Neurological manifestations can occur **even in the absence of anemia**.

## MRI Findings
- T2 hyperintensity in posterior columns of cervical and thoracic spinal cord
- **Inverted “V” sign** on axial MRI sections

## Diagnosis
- Low serum vitamin B12
- Elevated methylmalonic acid
- Elevated homocysteine
- Peripheral smear: macrocytosis, hypersegmented neutrophils

## Treatment
- Vitamin B12 replacement
- Intramuscular hydroxocobalamin or cyanocobalamin
- Early treatment can reverse symptoms
- Delayed treatment may halt progression, but deficits can become permanent

---

## High-Yield Exam Pearls
- Loss of vibration sense + spastic paraparesis → Think SCD
- Involvement of posterior columns + corticospinal tracts
- Nitrous oxide anesthesia can precipitate SCD
- Vitamin B12 deficiency causes **both anemia and neurological deficits** (folate deficiency does **not** cause neurological deficits)
