Two Types
1. Pyramidal Tracts- Voluntary 
2. Extrapyramidal - Involuntary

## Pyramidal Tracts
### 1. Lateral Corticospinal
Course: Lateral Funiculus (lemniscus?)
Decussates in Lower Medulla
Functions:
- Fine, skilled, voluntary movements
- Especially distal limb muscles (fingers = drama queens)

### 2. Anterior Corticospinal
Does NOT decussate in medulla —> Decussates segmentally near level of termination
Function: 
- Axial & proximal muscles (trunk)

## Extrapyramidal Tracts
### 1. Rubrospinal Tract 
1. Decussates Immediately
2. Functions:
	- Facilitate Flexors
	- Inhibits extensors
3. If intact —> Decorticate posture (Since red nucleus is the only one to facilitate Flexor)

### 2. Vestibulospinal Tract
#### a. Lateral Vestibulospinal
Do not decussate
Supply antigravity muscles (Extensor tone) —-> Help in Posture and stability

#### b. Medial Vestibulospinal
Ends in cervical cord
Supply Head and neck muscles

### 3. Reticulospinal Tracts
Both types act on extensors only. And their BALANCE is what maintains our balance (musle tone).
#### a. Medial (Pontine)
Facilitate extensors and increase tone
#### b. Lateral (Medullary)
Decrease tone of extensors

### 4. Tectospinal 
Origin from Superior colliculus
Helps in reflex turning toward visual/auditory stimuli
