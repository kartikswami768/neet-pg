Paramedian branches of Posterior cerebral artery

Affected structures: 
1. Occulomotor nerve fibers
2. Corticobulbar fibers
3. Corticospinal fibers
4. Substantia nigra

>Corticobulbar fibers supply face

Occulomotor fibers —> Do no cross —> Ipsilateral
Cortical fibers —> cross —> Contralateral

Substantia nigra produces Dopamine. Now Dopamine amount will decrease after weber’s. It will cause ***[[Parkinsonism]]*** on ***contralateral*** side

==**Contralateral Parkinsonism + Alternating hemiplegia:** ==
1. Ipsilateral CN III palsy (down and out)+ 
2. contralateral hemiplegia (Lower face + body)

Corticobulbar fibers may be involved in some cases causing paralysis of lower face

RED: Weber’s syndrome
Blue: [[Benedict’s syndrome]]
