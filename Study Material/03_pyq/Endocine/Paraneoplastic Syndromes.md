# 🔥 Rapid Revision Chart: Paraneoplastic Syndromes

---

## 1. ENDOCRINE PARANEOPLASTIC SYNDROMES

| Syndrome                            | Hormone | Associated Tumor                |
| ----------------------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------- |
| Cushing syndrome                    | ACTH    | Small cell lung carcinoma       |
| SIADH                               | ADH     | Small cell lung carcinoma       |
| Humoral hypercalcemia of malignancy | PTHrP   | Squamous cell carcinoma of lung |
| Hypoglycemia                        | IGF-2   | Mesenchymal tumors              |
| Polycythemia                        | EPO     | Renal cell carcinoma            |


## 2. NEUROLOGIC / NEUROMUSCULAR

| Syndrome | Feature | Tumor |
|----------|---------|-------|
| Lambert-Eaton myasthenic syndrome | Proximal muscle weakness | Small cell lung carcinoma |
| Paraneoplastic cerebellar degeneration | Ataxia | Ovarian, breast |
| Peripheral neuropathy | Sensory loss | Lung cancers |

## 3. DERMATOLOGICAL

| Syndrome | Feature | Tumor |
|----------|---------|-------|
| Acanthosis nigricans | Neck, axilla pigmentation | Gastric carcinoma |
| Dermatomyositis | Heliotrope rash | Lung, ovarian |
| Leser-Trélat sign | Multiple lesions | GI malignancy |

## 4. HEMATOLOGICAL / VASCULAR

| Syndrome | Feature | Tumor |
|----------|---------|-------|
| Trousseau syndrome | Migratory thrombophlebitis | Pancreatic cancer |
| Disseminated intravascular coagulation | Bleeding + clotting | Leukemia, prostate |
| Nonbacterial thrombotic endocarditis | Valve vegetations | Advanced malignancy |


## ⚡ ULTRA-FAST RECALL (1 MIN)

- Small cell lung carcinoma → ACTH + ADH + LEMS  
- Squamous cell carcinoma → PTHrP → Hypercalcemia  
- Renal cell carcinoma → EPO → Polycythemia  
- Pancreatic cancer → Trousseau syndrome  
- Gastric cancer → Acanthosis nigricans
