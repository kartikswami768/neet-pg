# Endocrine Glands and Their Hormones

### 1. Hypothalamus
   1. TRH  (also increase prolactin secretion)
   2. CRH  
   3. GnRH  
   4. GHRH  
   5. Somatostatin (GHIH)  
   6. Dopamine (PIH)  
### 2. Pituitary Gland 
#### 1. Anterior Pituitary 
   1. GH  
   2. Prolactin  (controlled by TRH and Dopamine)
   3. ACTH  
   4. TSH 
   5. FSH  
   6. LH  
#### 2. Posterior Pituitary
1. ADH (Vasopressin)  
2. Oxytocin  

### 3. Thyroid  
   1. T3 (Triiodothyronine)  
   2. T4 (Thyroxine)  
   3. Calcitonin  

### 4. Parathyroid  
   1. PTH (Parathyroid hormone)  

### 5. Adrenal Gland  
#### Cortex  
1. Zona glomerulosa → Aldosterone  
2. Zona fasciculata → Cortisol  
3. Zona reticularis → Androgens (DHEA)  
#### Medulla  
1. Epinephrine  
2. Norepinephrine  

### 6. Pancreas (Islets of Langerhans)  
   1. Insulin  
   2. Glucagon  
   3. Somatostatin  
   4. Pancreatic polypeptide  

### 7. Gonads  
   1. Testis  
      1. Testosterone  
      2. Inhibin B  
   2. Ovary  
      1. Estrogen  
      2. Progesterone  
      3. Inhibin  
      4. Relaxin  

### 8. Thymus  
   1. Thymosin  
   2. Thymopoietin  

### 9. Placenta  
   1. hCG  
   2. hPL  
   3. Estrogen  
   4. Progesterone  
   5. Relaxin  

### 10. Pineal  
1. Melatonin  

### 11. Other Endocrine Tissues

       1. Renin  
       2. Erythropoietin  
       3. Calcitriol  
##### 2. Heart  
       1. ANP  
       2. BNP  
##### 3. Liver  
       1. IGF-1  
       2. Angiotensinogen  
       3. Thrombopoietin  
##### 4. GIT  
       1. Gastrin  
       2. Secretin  
       3. CCK  
       4. GIP  
       5. Motilin  
       6. Ghrelin  
##### 5. Adipose Tissue  
       1. Leptin  
       2. Adiponectin
