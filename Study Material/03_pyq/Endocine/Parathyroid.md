**Development:** Third pharyngeal pouch, migrate with thymus.
--> MOST COMMON ECTOPIC PARATHYROID: Mediastenum (and that too inferior parathyroid)

**PATHOLOGY**
1. MEN 1 Syndrome: Parathyroid adenoma is the most common manifestation
2. PTH-related peptide: most common cause of hypercalcemia in malignance. It is mostly secreted by SCC (mostly lung), RCC, Breast cancer
