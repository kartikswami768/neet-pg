- **Before Thyroid Storm, What is Thyrotoxicosis?**
	Thyrotoxicosis is basically high levels of thyroid hormones.
	It can be due to hyperthyroidism or not.
# Difference between Thyrotoxicosis and Thyroid Storm
Thyroid Storm is Thyrotoxicosis with Decompensation.
- **Why Decompensation happened?**
	Because of a trigger, like, infection, surgery, abrupt stoppage of antithyroid drugs or something else. adrenergic effects of thyrotoxicosis shot up. It stimulated organs to do so much work that they actually cannot. So every organ will be going into failure.
- **What happened due to decompensation?**
	Almost every organ went into failure
	- Temp: >40 degree celsius
	- CNS: agitation, altered sensorium, delirium, coma
	- CVS: Atriial fibrillation, Severe tachycardia, Hart failure, Shock
	- Liver failure
	- Adrenal failure
## Management
1. Control adrenergic activity
	beta blockers (like propanolol)
	If asthamatic, then Dilitiazem or virapamil (CCB)
	>They also help in controlling atrial fibrillation
	
2. Start anti-thyroid drugs
	carbimazole/methimazole - Thyrotoxicosis
	PTU - Thyroid Storm 
	
3. Give iodine

**In case of thyroid storm**
4. give Hydrocort to support adrenals
