# 🧬 Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia (MEN)

**Definition:**  
Inherited disorders causing tumors in multiple endocrine glands  
**TUMOUR IS PRODUCED ONLY IN ENDOCRINAL TISSUE, NOT IN “ANY KIND OF TISSUE” IN THE GLAND**

**Inheritance:**  
- Autosomal dominant  


# 🔴 MEN 1 (Wermer syndrome)

**Gene:** MEN1 (menin) – tumor suppressor  

## 🎯 “3 Ps”
- Parathyroid → hyperplasia/adenoma (most common)
- Pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors (NETs)
- Pituitary tumors  

## 🧠 Details

### 1. Parathyroid
- Primary hyperparathyroidism  
- ↑ Ca²⁺, ↓ PO₄³⁻  
- Earliest + most common feature  
### 2. Pancreatic NETs
- Gastrinoma (Zollinger–Ellison syndrome)  
- Insulinoma  
- VIPoma, glucagonoma  
### 3. Pituitary
- Prolactinoma (most common)  
- GH-secreting tumor  
- (**ALWAYS ANTERIOR PITUITARY, AS POSTERIOR IS NOT ENDOCRINAL TISSUE**)
## 💡 Clue
- Multiple endocrine tumors + hypercalcemia → think MEN1  

# 🟠 MEN 2A (Sipple syndrome)

**Gene:** RET proto-oncogene  

## 🎯 Triad
- Medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) (almost 100%)  
- Pheochromocytoma  
- Parathyroid hyperplasia  

## 🧠 Notes
- MTC → ↑ calcitonin  
- Always screen for pheochromocytoma before thyroid surgery  

# 🟡 MEN 2B

**Gene:** RET (more aggressive mutation)  

## 🎯 Features
- Medullary thyroid carcinoma (early, aggressive)  
- Pheochromocytoma  
- No parathyroid involvement  

## 🌟 Unique features
- Mucosal neuromas (lips, tongue)  
- Marfanoid habitus  

---

# ⚡ Key Differences

| Feature | MEN 1 | MEN 2A | MEN 2B |
|--------|------|--------|--------|
| Gene | MEN1 | RET | RET |
| Parathyroid | ✔️ | ✔️ | ❌ |
| Pancreatic NETs | ✔️ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Pituitary | ✔️ | ❌ | ❌ |
| MTC | ❌ | ✔️ | ✔️ (early, aggressive) |
| Pheochromocytoma | ❌ | ✔️ | ✔️ |
| Neuromas | ❌ | ❌ | ✔️ |

# 🧠 Quick Recall

- MEN 1 → 3 Ps  
- MEN 2A → MTC + Pheo + Parathyroid  
- MEN 2B → MTC + Pheo + Neuromas (no parathyroid)  

---

# ⚠️ Clinical Pearls

- MEN2 → Prophylactic thyroidectomy (based on RET mutation)  
- MEN1 → Monitor calcium first  
- Always rule out pheochromocytoma before surgery
