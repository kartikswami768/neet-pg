# Hematology Image Recognition

### Acute Promyelocytic Leukemia (APL)

**Image clue:**
- Promyelocytes with abundant granules
- Multiple Auer rods ("Faggot cells")

**Genetics:**
- t(15;17)
- PML-RARA

**Buzzword:**
- DIC
- All-trans retinoic acid (ATRA)


### Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML)

**Image clue:**
- Myeloblasts with Auer rods

**Genetics:**
- t(8;21)
- inv(16)
- t(15;17) (APL subtype)

**Buzzword:**
- Auer rods = AML until proven otherwise

---

### Chronic Myeloid Leukemia (CML)

**Image clue:**
- Granulocytes at all stages of maturation
- Myelocytes, metamyelocytes, neutrophils together

**Genetics:**
- t(9;22)
- BCR-ABL
- Philadelphia chromosome

---

### Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL)

**Image clue:**
- Smudge cells

**Association:**
- Elderly patient
- Absolute lymphocytosis

---

### Hairy Cell Leukemia

**Image clue:**
- Lymphocytes with hair-like cytoplasmic projections

**Genetics:**
- BRAF mutation

---

### Hodgkin Lymphoma

**Image clue:**
- Reed-Sternberg cells
- Owl-eye appearance

---

### Burkitt Lymphoma

**Image clue:**
- Starry-sky appearance

**Genetics:**
- t(8;14)
- MYC

---

### Follicular Lymphoma

**Image clue:**
- Nodular/follicular growth pattern

**Genetics:**
- t(14;18)
- BCL2

---

### Mantle Cell Lymphoma

**Genetics:**
- t(11;14)
- Cyclin D1 (CCND1)

---
