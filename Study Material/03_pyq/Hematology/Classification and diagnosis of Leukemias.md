# Flow Cytometry
This is what we need to make diagnosis of almost every Leukemia. Except CML.
## Why don’t we need FLOW Cytometry to make diagnosis of CML?
Because every case of CML has philadelphia chromosome. CML is defined by Philadelphia chromosome. If philadelphia chromosome is there with proliferative myeloid lineage (which is mostly mature) it IS CML.

## Then why do we need it for CLL?
First of all, CLL and CML are not at all equivalent.
Remember we are talking about Chronic Leukemia here, which have mostly mature cells. That means a lot of Neutrophils, band cells, basophils, eosinophils in case of CML and a lot of B-cells and T-cells in case of CLL. Right?
Wrong. The first part of that statement (about CML) right but the later is wrong. 

> CLL is just about B-cells. Clonal proliferation of B-cells

And there lies the problem that cannot be solved with immunophenotyping AKA Flow Cytometry. Flow cytometry tells us which lineage does this particular lymphocyte belongs to. Is it peripheral B cell, T cell or any other particular Lymphocyte.

### So what about T-Cell proliferation? 
There are 6 T cell clonal proliferation neoplasms described in Robbins. It can be anyone of them.

### So Does Clonal B-Cell proliferation means CLL?
NO. It can be Follicular lymphoma, burkitt lymphoma, hairy cell leukemia, mantle cell lymphoma, DLBCL(Lymphoma), Marginal Zone Lymphoma. Pay attention all these are lymphoma except Hairy cell Leukemia. This Lymphoma can spill over to blood to create a similar picture as that of CLL
