# Pacemaker
Maintains Heart rate in cases of Bradyarrhythmia
Do not give shock, just an impulse at a regular interval
Use:
1. Sinus node Dysfunction
2. AV Block
# Implantable Cardioverter-Defibrillator
Can detect Arrhythmia.
Can give shock.
it can be automatic, that is, can detect and provide shock automatically.
Use:
1. Brugada Syndrome
2. Post-MI low EF
3. Some cardiomyopathies
4. VT/VF
# Cardiac Resynchronisation Therapy
Synchronise contraction of Left and Right Heart.
Can be combined with ICD.
Use: Heart failure with 
- Wide QRS
- Low EF
- Dyssynchrony
