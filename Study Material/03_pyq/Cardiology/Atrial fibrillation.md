
# Atrial Fibrillation (AF) – NEET PG High-Yield Points

## 1. Definition & ECG
- **Irregularly irregular rhythm**
- **No P waves**
- **Fibrillatory (f) waves**
- **Variable R–R interval**

📌 AF ≠ Atrial flutter  
- Flutter → saw-tooth F waves, usually regular ventricular rate

---

## 2. Causes (Very Commonly Asked)
**Mitral + Thyroid + Heart disease**
- Mitral stenosis (classic)
- Ischemic heart disease
- Hypertension
- Heart failure
- Thyrotoxicosis
- Alcohol (Holiday heart)
- Post-cardiac surgery

---

## 3. Types of AF
- **Paroxysmal**: < 7 days, self-terminating  
- **Persistent**: > 7 days, needs cardioversion  
- **Long-standing persistent**: > 1 year  
- **Permanent AF**: rhythm control abandoned

📌 AF for 3–4 days → still *paroxysmal*

---

## 4. Complications
👉 Thrombus usually forms in **left atrial appendage**

---

## 6. Cardioversion Rules
- **AF < 48 hours** → cardioversion allowed directly
- **AF > 48 hours / unknown duration** →  
  - Anticoagulate for **3 weeks** OR  
  - **TEE-guided cardioversion**

TEE looks for → **LA appendage thrombus, size of LA**

# Management
There are two options for here when we go for pharmacological intervention
### 1. DC cardioversion
Unstable patient
OR when AF has persisted for more than 48 hours/unknown duration (can do pharmacological cardioversion in this case)

### 2. Rhythm control (Pharmacological Cardioversion)
- It restores sinus rhythm. Used when chances of recurrence are less as rhythm control is difficult to maintain.
- **Ibutilide -** Normal Heart Structure
- **Amiodarone-** Abnormal Heart structure
- Indication:
	- <48 hours AF
	- >48 hours/unknow duration (can do DC cardioversion also)
	- Young patient
	- Symptomatic

### 3. Rate control
- It controls ventricular rate.
- It ***DOES NOT control AF***. AF will still be there with controlled ventricular contraction rate.
- **Beta blocker**
- **CCB (Diltiazem, virapamil)**
- **Digoxin (HF + sedentary)**
- Indication: When it is harder to maintain rhythm. And that happens when there is high chance of recurrence.
	1. Permanent AF
	2. Older patients >65 years
	3. Minimal sympyoms
	4. Long standing AF
	5. High recurrence risk (enlarged LA, RHD)
	6. Contraindications to antiarrhythmic drugs

---

## 7. Anticoagulation – CHA₂DS₂-VASc Score
- C: CHF – 1
- H: Hypertension – 1
- A: Age ≥75 – 2
- D: Diabetes – 1
- S: Stroke/TIA – 2
- V: Vascular disease – 1
- A: Age 65–74 – 1
- Sc: Sex (female) – 1

📌 Score ≥ 2 → **Anticoagulation indicated**

### Drugs
- Warfarin
- DOACs (apixaban, rivaroxaban, dabigatran)

⚠️ **Mitral stenosis / Mechanical valve** → **ONLY warfarin**

---

## 8. AF with WPW (Exam Favorite)
❌ **Do NOT use**
- Digoxin
- Verapamil
- Diltiazem
- β-blockers

✅ **Use**
- Procainamide
- Amiodarone
- DC cardioversion

Reason → AV node blockers may cause **VF**

---

## 9. AF in Thyrotoxicosis
- Rate control → **β-blockers (propranolol)**
- Rhythm often normalizes after treating thyroid state

📌 Causes **hyperthermia**, not fever

---

## 10. One-Liners for NEET PG
- Most common sustained arrhythmia → **AF**
- Most common cause of embolic stroke → **AF**
- AF + Mitral stenosis → **Highest stroke risk**
- Thrombus site → **Left atrial appendage**
