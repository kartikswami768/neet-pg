---

## **Staphylococcus aureus** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Most common organism overall**
- **Acute infective endocarditis**
- **Normal valves**
- **IV drug users → tricuspid valve → Septi pulmonary emboli → Cavitating lung lesions**
- Rapid valve destruction  
👉 *Toxic patient + fast course*

---

## **Viridans Streptococci** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Most common cause of subacute IE**
- **Previously damaged valves**
- **Prosthetic valve IE**
	- **Late onset**
- **Dental caries / dental procedures**
- Indolent course  
👉 *Dental history + murmur*

---

## **Staphylococcus epidermidis** ⭐⭐⭐
Coagulase negative Staph.
- **Prosthetic valve IE**
	- **Early onset (< 2 months post-surgery)**
- **Pacemaker / ICD-related IE**
- Biofilm formation  
👉 *Foreign body present*

---

## **Enterococcus species** ⭐⭐
- **Post GI / GU procedures**
- Elderly patients
- Intrinsic drug resistance  
👉 *Colonoscopy / TURP history*

---

## **Streptococcus bovis (S. gallolyticus)** ⭐⭐
- **Associated with colon cancer**
- Subacute IE  
👉 *Mandatory colonoscopy*

---

## **HACEK organisms** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Culture-negative IE**
- Fastidious Gram-negative bacilli  
  - Haemophilus  
  - Aggregatibacter  
  - Cardiobacterium  
  - Eikenella  
  - Kingella  
👉 *Repeated negative blood cultures*

---

## **Fungal Endocarditis** ⭐
- **Candida**
- **Aspergillus**
- Prosthetic valves
- IV drug users
- Immunocompromised
- Large vegetations, high embolic risk  
👉 *Severe, refractory IE*

---

## **Other Culture-Negative Causes**
- Coxiella burnetii
- Bartonella
- Brucella

---

## ⚡ **10-Second NEET Recall**
- **Acute + normal valve → Staph aureus**
- **Dental source + damaged valve → Viridans**
- **Prosthetic valve (early) → Staph epidermidis**
- **GI / GU procedure → Enterococcus**
- **Colon cancer → Strep bovis**
- **Culture-negative → HACEK**
