### Causes
- Young: Bicuspid aortic valve  
- Elderly: Degenerative calcification  
- Rheumatic (developing countries)

### Pathology
Narrowed aortic valve → LV pressure overload → Concentric LV hypertrophy  

### Classic Triad (SAD)
- Syncope  
- Angina  
- Dyspnea  

### Auscultation
- Ejection systolic murmur (crescendo-decrescendo)
- Right 2nd intercostal space  
- Radiates to carotids  
- Pulsus parvus et tardus (slow rising pulse)

**Buzzword:** Ejection systolic murmur → Carotids
