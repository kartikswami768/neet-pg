**Most common cause:** Rheumatic heart disease  
**Typical patient:** Young female with past rheumatic fever  

### Pathology
Mitral narrowing → ↑ LA pressure → LA enlargement → Pulmonary congestion → Pulmonary HTN → RV failure  

### Clinical Features
- Dyspnea (most common)
- Hemoptysis
- Atrial fibrillation
- Malar flush

### Auscultation
- Loud S1  
- Opening snap  
- Mid-diastolic rumbling murmur at apex  

**Why Opening Snap?**
- LA pressure is very high (because blood is backed up).
- The stenosed valve leaflets are thickened but still mobile.
- The high LA pressure forces the stiff valve open suddenly.


**Buzzword:** Opening snap + mid-diastolic murmur
