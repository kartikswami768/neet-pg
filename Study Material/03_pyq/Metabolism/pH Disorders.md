# [[Compensation in pH Disorders]]

--------------------------------------------------
### METABOLIC DISORDERS → RESPIRATORY COMPENSATION
--------------------------------------------------

1. Metabolic Acidosis (Winter’s Formula):
   Expected PaCO₂ = (1.5 × HCO₃⁻) + 8 ± 2

2. Metabolic Alkalosis:
   Expected PaCO₂ = 40 + 0.7 × (HCO₃⁻ − 24) ± 2


--------------------------------------------------
### RESPIRATORY DISORDERS → METABOLIC COMPENSATION
--------------------------------------------------

3. Respiratory Acidosis:
   Acute:   HCO₃⁻ ↑ by 1 mEq/L for every ↑10 mmHg PaCO₂
   Chronic: HCO₃⁻ ↑ by 4 mEq/L for every ↑10 mmHg PaCO₂

4. Respiratory Alkalosis:
   Acute:   HCO₃⁻ ↓ by 2 mEq/L for every ↓10 mmHg PaCO₂
   Chronic: HCO₃⁻ ↓ by 5 mEq/L for every ↓10 mmHg PaCO₂


--------------------------------------------------
## ANION GAP & MIXED DISORDER TOOLS
--------------------------------------------------

5. Anion Gap (AG):
   AG = Na⁺ − (Cl⁻ + HCO₃⁻)
   Normal: 8–12 mEq/L

6. Delta Gap (ΔAG):
   ΔAG = AG − 12

7. Delta Bicarbonate (ΔHCO₃⁻):
   ΔHCO₃⁻ = 24 − HCO₃⁻

8. Delta–Delta Interpretation:
   If ΔAG ≈ ΔHCO₃⁻ → Pure high AG metabolic acidosis
   If ΔAG > ΔHCO₃⁻ → Concurrent metabolic alkalosis
   If ΔAG < ΔHCO₃⁻ → Concurrent normal AG metabolic acidosis
