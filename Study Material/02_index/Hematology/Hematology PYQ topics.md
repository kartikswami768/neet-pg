### 2. Strategic Insights

* **INICET Dynamics (Conceptual/Integrated):**
  * Focuses heavily on *Laboratory Medicine*, *Pre-analytical Variables*, and *Pathophysiology*.
  * Integrates Clinical Therapeutics with Molecular Pathology (e.g., ADAMTS13 autoantibodies in TTP, TKI resistance mechanics in CML, TEG parameters in emergency resuscitation).
  * Prefers multi-step diagnostic questions (e.g., differentiating Thalassemia Trait from IDA using complete CBC indices + Ferritin, rather than simple Hb levels).
* **NEET PG Dynamics (Factual/Recall):**
  * Focuses on *Classic Morphological "Key Words"* (e.g., "Starry sky", "Faggot cells", "Popcorn cells", "Pencil cells").
  * Direct chromosomal translocation questions ($t(11;14), t(15;17), t(8;14)$).
  * High emphasis on direct pharmacological facts (e.g., Warfarin mechanism on $\gamma$-carboxylation, Antidotes).

## Exam Priority List

### ★★★★★ Must Master (Highest Yield)
* **Coagulation Cascade & Bleeding Disorders:** Extensively tested across all exams. Master PT/aPTT pathways, vWD, Hemophilia A/B/XIII, and Anticoagulants/Antidotes.
* **Acute Leukemias (APML Focus) & CML:** APML ($t(15;17)$) and CML ($t(9;22)$) appear almost every single exam cycle.
* **Microcytic Hypochromic Anemias:** Differentiating IDA, Thalassemia Trait, Anemia of Chronic Disease, and Sideroblastic Anemia using indices, Ferritin, and HPLC.
* **Hemolytic Anemias (Sickle Cell, G6PD, PNH):** High density of genetics, peripheral smear findings, and management protocols.

### ★★★★ Very Important
* **Lymphomas (Hodgkin & Non-Hodgkin):** Chromosomal translocations ($t(8;14), t(14;18), t(11;14)$) and immunohistochemical markers ($CD15, CD30, CD10, BCL2$).
* **Megaloblastic Anemias:** Vitamin $B_{12}$ vs Folate differentiation, absorption sites, neurological sequelae, and peripheral blood features.
* **Transfusion Medicine & Blood Products:** Storage durations, Blood Group Antigens, Massive Transfusion complications, and Transfusion Reactions.

### ★★★ Important
* **Plasma Cell Disorders (Multiple Myeloma):** Bone marrow aspirate morphology, M-spike analysis, CRAB criteria, and secondary Amyloidosis.
* **Laboratory Diagnostics & Vacutainers:** Tube color codes, Flow Cytometry applications, Reticulocyte count correction, and Bone Marrow needles.

### ★★ Moderate
* **Aplastic Anemia & Bone Marrow Failure:** Causes of "Dry Tap", association of Pure Red Cell Aplasia with Thymoma, and absence of splenomegaly.
* **Myeloproliferative Neoplasms (PV, Myelofibrosis):** $JAK2\ V617F$ mutation and myelofibrosis management.

### ★ Low Yield
* **Rare Immunodeficiencies & Vasculitis:** Bare Lymphocyte Syndrome, Wiskott-Aldrich Syndrome, and systemic vasculitis manifestations (Behcet's, GPA).

## Last Minute Revision Checklist

- [ ] **APML:** $t(15;17)$, $PML\text{-}RARA$, Auer Rods / Faggot Cells, ATRA response, Risk of DIC.
- [ ] **CML:** $t(9;22)$, $BCR\text{-}ABL1$, Low LAP Score, Splenomegaly, Imatinib therapy.
- [ ] **Burkitt Lymphoma:** $t(8;14)$, $c\text{-}MYC$, Starry-sky pattern, EBV association, Jaw mass.
- [ ] **Follicular Lymphoma:** $t(14;18)$, $BCL2$ overexpression, $CD10^+$.
- [ ] **Mantle Cell Lymphoma:** $t(11;14)$, Cyclin D1, $SOX11^+$.
- [ ] **Hodgkin Lymphoma:** Classical ($CD15^+, CD30^+$) vs NLPHL (Popcorn cells, $CD20^+, CD15^-, CD30^-$).
- [ ] **IDA vs Thalassemia Trait:** Mentzer Index $< 13$ (Thalassemia), Ferritin low in IDA.
- [ ] **G6PD Deficiency:** X-linked, Heinz Bodies, Bite Cells, Oxidative triggers, Normal PT/aPTT.
- [ ] **PNH:** $PIGA$ gene mutation, $CD55/CD59$ deficiency on Flow Cytometry, Venous Thrombosis (Budd-Chiari).
- [ ] **Sickle Cell Anemia:** Glutamic Acid $\rightarrow$ Valine (6th position of $\beta$-chain), Hydroxyurea increases $HbF$.
- [ ] **vWD vs Hemophilia:** vWD = Autosomal Dominant, prolonged BT & aPTT; Hemophilia = X-linked, prolonged aPTT only.
- [ ] **Factor XIII Deficiency:** Normal PT and aPTT, abnormal Urea Clot Solubility test, Umbilical bleeding.
- [ ] **Anticoagulants & Antidotes:** Heparin (Protamine), Warfarin (Vit K / PCC), Dabigatran (Idarucizumab).
- [ ] **Vacutainer Colors:** Light Blue (Sodium Citrate - Coagulation), Green (Heparin - Electrolytes/ABG), Purple (EDTA - CBC).
- [ ] **Blood Storage:** PRBC = 35–42 days ($2\text{--}6^\circ\text{C}$), Platelets = 5 days ($20\text{--}24^\circ\text{C}$ with agitation), FFP = 1 year ($-18^\circ\text{C}$).
- [ ] **Massive Transfusion:** Hypocalcemia (Citrate toxicity), Hyperkalemia, Hypothermia.
