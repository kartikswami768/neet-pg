# Parenteral drugs used in hypertensive emergencies

| Class | Drugs | Contraindications |
|---|---|---|
| Beta-blockers | Labetalol, esmolol, metoprolol | Heart block, systolic heart failure, asthma, bradycardia |
| Calcium channel blockers | Clevidipine, nicardipine | Advanced aortic stenosis |
| D1 receptor agonist | Fenoldopam | Caution in glaucoma |
| Vasodilators | Nitroglycerin; Nitroprusside; Hydralazine | Pericardial tamponade; relatively contraindicated in liver failure and renal failure; CAD, RHD |
| ACE inhibitors | Enalaprilat | History of angioedema |
| Alpha-blocker | Phentolamine, Urapidil | Aortic stenosis, AV shunt |
| Centrally acting sympatholytics | Clonidine | Severe AV block, sick-sinus syndrome |
