  # Cellcycle specific and non-specific cytotoxic agents

  Pearl ID: PMO914

  #flashcards

  **What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Cellcycle specific and non-specific cytotoxic agents" (PMO914)?**

  ?

  ### Cell-cycle-specific agents

  - Antimetabolites: **S phase**
  - Camptothecin analogs: **S phase**
  - Taxanes, Vinca alkaloids: **M phase**
  - Epipodophyllotoxin: **G1-S phase**
  - Bleomycin: **G2-M phase**

  ### Cell-cycle-non-specific agents

  - Alkylating agents
  - Platinum analogs
  - Anthracyclines
  - Mitomycin, dactinomycin
