# Beta-blockers with vasodilating properties

The beta-blockers with vasodilating property are:

- Carvedilol
- Labetalol
- Bucindolol
- Celiprolol
- Nebivolol
- Nipradilol
- Carteolol
- Betaxolol
- Bopindolol
- Bevantolol
