#review

**What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Ceiling effect of opioids" (PM0517)?**

?

| Drug | Correlation of respiratory depression with dose |
|---|---|
| Morphine | Increases proportionally with dose (i.e. no ceiling effect) |
| Buprenorphine | Ceiling effect at 0.15-1.2 mg in adults |
| Butorphanol | Ceiling effect at 30-60 μg/kg |
| Nalbuphine | Ceiling effect at 30 mg in adults |
| Pentazocine | Ceiling effect suggested |
