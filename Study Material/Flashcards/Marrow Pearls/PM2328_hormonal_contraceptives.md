# Hormonal contraceptives

Pearl ID: PM2328

#flashcards

**What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Hormonal contraceptives" (PM2328)?**

?
### Oral pills

#### Combined oral contraceptive pills (COC)
- Mala-D — Levonorgestrel 0.15 mg + ethinyl estradiol 0.03 mg + ferrous fumarate
- Mala-N — Levonorgestrel 0.15 mg + ethinyl estradiol 0.03 mg + ferrous fumarate (Government supply, free of cost)

#### Progesterone-only pills (POP)
Suitable for lactating women:
- Norethisterone 350 µg
- Norgestrel 75 µg
- Levonorgestrel (LNG) 30 µg

#### Once-a-month or long-acting pills
- Quinestrol (long-acting estrogen) + short-acting progesterone
- Gossypol — male pill; cottonseed derivative and causes azoospermia and severe oligospermia

### Post-coital pills / interceptive
- Levonorgestrel 0.75 mg, 2 doses, 12 hours apart
- Mifepristone 10–600 mg
- Ulipristal 30 mg, within 5 days of intercourse
- Centchroman 60 mg, 2 tablets, within 24 hours of intercourse
- GnRH agonists
- CuT IUCD, inserted within 5 days

### Injectable contraceptives

#### Progesterone-only injectables
- DMPA 150 mg IM every 3 months
- NET-EN 200 mg IM every 2 months

#### Combined injectables
- Cyclofem — DMPA 25 mg + estradiol cypionate 5 mg
- Mesigyna — NET-EN 50 mg + estradiol valerate 5 mg

### Subdermal implants
- Norplant I — levonorgestrel 35 mg
- Norplant II or Jadelle — 2 rods with levonorgestrel 70 mg in each, released at a rate of 50 µg/day

### Other hormonal methods
- Hormonal IUCD — Mirena, contains LNG 52 mg with release rate of 20 µg/day
- Ortho Evra patch — norelgestomin + ethinyl estradiol weekly for 3 weeks, then patch-free for 1 week
- Vaginal ring / Nuvaring — etonogestrel + ethinyl estradiol daily release; use for 3 weeks with withdrawal for 1 week
