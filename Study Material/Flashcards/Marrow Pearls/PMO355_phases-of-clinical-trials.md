# Phases of Clinical Trials

|  | **Phase I** (First in human) | **Phase II** (First in patient) | **Phase III** (Multi-site trial) | **Phase IV** (Post-marketing) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Participants | 10–100 | 50–500 | 500–3000 | Large or according to study |
| Population | Usually healthy volunteers | Patients with the target disease | Patients with the target disease | Real-world patients |
| Design | Open-label study | Randomized and controlled trial; may be blinded | Randomized and controlled trial; may be blinded | Usually observational or open-label |
| Purpose | **Human pharmacology and safety:** To determine safety, tolerability and pharmacokinetic parameters | **Therapeutic exploration and dose-ranging:** To determine efficacy and dose-ranging | **Therapeutic confirmation and comparison:** Indications are finalized and guidelines for therapeutic use are formulated. Monitoring of adverse reactions is done. | **Postmarketing surveillance:** To monitor drug interactions, compliance and long-term or rare side effects |
| Duration | Extends from months to 1 year | 1–2 years | 3–5 years | Unending, after drug approval |
