# Basics of autonomic nervous system

| Feature | Para-sympathetic system | Sympathetic system |
|---|---|---|
| Outflow | Cranio-sacral; cranial nerve nuclei III, VII, IX, X; sacral segments S2 to S4 | Thoraco-lumbar; T1–L2 |
| Pre-ganglionic receptor | N<sub>n</sub> (nicotinic neuronal) | N<sub>n</sub> |
| Pre-ganglionic neurotransmitter (from CNS) | Acetylcholine | Acetylcholine |
| Post-ganglionic receptors | Muscarinic or Nicotinic | Alpha or Beta |
| Post-ganglionic neurotransmitter (to the organ) | Acetylcholine | Nor-epinephrine; exceptions: renal system—Dopamine; sweat and adrenal glands—Acetylcholine |
| Features | The ganglion is located closer to the effector organ; pre-ganglionic fibers are longer | The ganglion is located closer to the spinal cord; post-ganglionic fibers are longer | 

> **Note:** The nerve roots arising from the CNS are all nicotinic cholinergic.
