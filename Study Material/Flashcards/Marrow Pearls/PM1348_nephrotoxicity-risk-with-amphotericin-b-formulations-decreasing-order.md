# Nephrotoxicity risk with Amphotericin-B formulations (decreasing order)

Pearl ID: PM1348

#flashcards

**What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Nephrotoxicity risk with Amphotericin-B formulations (decreasing order)" (PM1348)?**

?

The source Pearl states:

- Conventional amphotericin B (**C-AMB**) has the highest risk of nephrotoxicity.
- Decreasing order: **C-AMB > Amphotericin B lipid complex > Liposomal amphotericin B**.
- Amphotericin B colloidal dispersion has not been compared with other lipid-based formulations.
