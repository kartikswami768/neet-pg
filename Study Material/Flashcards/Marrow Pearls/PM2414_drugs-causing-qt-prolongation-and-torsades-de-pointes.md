# Drugs causing QT prolongation and torsades de pointes

### Antiarrhythmics

- Sotalol
- Amiodarone
- Quinidine
- Procainamide
- Dofetilide
- Ibutilide

### Antimicrobials

- Levofloxacin
- Ciprofloxacin
- Gatifloxacin
- Moxifloxacin
- Clarithromycin
- Erythromycin
- Ketoconazole
- Itraconazole
- Rilpivirine

### Antidepressants

- Amitriptyline
- Desipramine
- Imipramine
- Doxepin
- Fluoxetine
- Sertraline
- Venlafaxine

### Antipsychotics

- Haloperidol
- Droperidol
- Quetiapine
- Ziprasidone
- Thioridazine

### Others

- Cisapride
- Sumatriptan
- Zolmitriptan
- Arsenic
- Dolasetron
- Methadone
