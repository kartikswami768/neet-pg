# Ipratropium vs Tiotropium

| Feature | Ipratropium | Tiotropium |
|---|---|---|
| Duration of action | Short | Long |
| Frequency of administration | 3–4 times/day | Once daily |
| Antimuscarinic action | Non-selective | Relatively selective for M1 and M3 receptors |
| Paradoxical bronchoconstriction | Present* | Absent |

\* Attributed to the nebulizer solution and other additives added. Blockade of prejunctional M2 receptors that normally inhibit acetylcholine release also can contribute.
