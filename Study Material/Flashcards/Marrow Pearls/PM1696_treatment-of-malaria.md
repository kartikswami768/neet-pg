# Treatment of Malaria

Pearl ID: PM1696

#flashcards

**What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Treatment of Malaria" (PM1696)?**

?

| Clinical situation | Drug of choice | Primaquine dose |
|---|---|---|
| **P. vivax malaria** | Chloroquine 25 mg/kg divided as: 10 mg/kg day 1, 10 mg/kg day 2, 5 mg/kg day 3 | 0.25 mg/kg for 14 days |
| **P. falciparum — North East** | ACT-AL for 3 days | 0.75 mg/kg on day 2 |
| **P. falciparum — other states** | ACT-SP for 3 days | 0.75 mg/kg on day 2 |
| **Mixed infection — North East** | ACT-AL for 3 days | 0.25 mg/kg for 14 days |
| **Mixed infection — other states** | ACT-SP for 3 days | 0.25 mg/kg for 14 days |
| **Resistance to ACT** | Quinine | — |
| **Severe malaria** | IV Artesunate | — |
| **Pregnancy — P. vivax** | Chloroquine | Contraindicated |
| **Pregnancy — P. falciparum** | 1st trimester: Quinine; 2nd and 3rd trimester: area-specific ACT | Contraindicated |

**ACT:** artemisinin-based combination therapy  
**AL:** artemether-lumefantrine  
**SP:** sulfadoxine-pyrimethamine
