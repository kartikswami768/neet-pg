  **Drugs that precipitate gout**

  - Cyclosporine or cancer drugs
  - Aspirin (low dose)
  - Niacin
  - Thiazide
  - Lasix (furosemide)
  - Ethambutol
  - Alcohol
  - Pyrazinamide

  **Mnemonic:** `CANT LEAP`
