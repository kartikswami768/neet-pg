# Phases of drug metabolism

### Phase I reactions

They attach functional groups to the drugs. This usually leads to the inactivation of the drug.

Examples:

- Oxidation
- Reduction
- Hydrolysis
- Cyclisation
- Deamination

### Phase II reactions

They attach conjugates to the drugs. This facilitates drug elimination from tissue.

Examples:

- Glucuronide conjugation
- Sulfate conjugation
- Glycine conjugation
- Glutathione conjugation
- Acetylation
- Methylation

**All phase II reactions are mainly conducted by the non-microsomal enzymes except glucuronidation.**
