### Mechanism of action

The Marrow diagram summarizes the major antifungal targets:

- **Griseofulvin** → inhibits **microtubule assembly**.
- **Flucytosine** → inhibits **DNA and RNA synthesis**.
- **Terbinafine** → inhibits the **squalene epoxidase** step.
- **Azoles** → inhibit **ergosterol synthesis**.
- **Amphotericin B and nystatin** → act on the **fungal cell membrane**.
- **Echinocandins** → inhibit **1,3-beta-D-glucan synthase**, affecting the fungal cell wall.
