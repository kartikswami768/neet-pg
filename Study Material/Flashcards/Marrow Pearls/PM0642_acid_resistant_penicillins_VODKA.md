# Acid resistant penicillins: mnemonic "VODKA"

Pearl ID: PM0642

#flashcards

**What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "Acid resistant penicillins: mnemonic "VODKA"" (PM0642)?**

?

The mnemonic **VODKA** can be used for acid-resistant penicillins that are active orally.

| Letter | Drug(s) in the pearl |
|---|---|
| V | Penicillin V |
| O | Oxacillin |
| D | Dicloxacillin |
| K | Cloxacillin |
| A | Amoxycillin / Ampicillin |
