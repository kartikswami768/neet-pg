# Muscarinic and Nicotinic Signs and symptoms of Organophosphorus Poisoning

| Mechanism | Site affected | Symptoms |
|---|---|---|
| Muscarinic | Exocrine glands | Salivation, lacrimation, perspiration |
| Muscarinic | Eyes | Miosis, blurred vision |
| Muscarinic | GIT | Abdominal cramps, vomiting, diarrhea |
| Muscarinic | Respiratory tract | Increased secretions, bronchoconstriction |
| Muscarinic | Bladder | Urinary frequency, incontinence |
| Muscarinic | CVS | Bradycardia, hypotension |
| Nicotinic | Skeletal muscles | Muscle fasciculations, flaccid paralysis |
| Nicotinic | CVS (through cholinergic stimulation of sympathetic ganglia) | Tachycardia, transient hypertension |
| Muscarinic and nicotinic | CNS | Dizziness, lethargy, fatigue, headache, mental confusion, depression of respiratory centres, convulsions, coma |
