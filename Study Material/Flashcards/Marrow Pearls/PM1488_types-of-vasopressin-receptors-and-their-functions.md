# Types of vasopressin receptors and their functions

| Receptor | Localization | Functions |
|---|---|---|
| V<sub>1a</sub> | Vascular smooth muscle; Platelets; Hepatocytes; Myometrium | Vasoconstriction; Platelet aggregation; Glycogenolysis; Uterine contraction |
| V<sub>3</sub>/V<sub>1b</sub> | Anterior pituitary | ACTH release |
| V<sub>2</sub> | Basolateral membrane of collecting tubule; Vascular endothelium; Vascular smooth muscle | Upregulation of Aqp2; vWF & factor 8 release; Vasodilation |
