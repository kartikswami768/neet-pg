# Dialyzable and non-dialyzable drugs

### Common dialyzable drugs

- Barbiturates
- Lithium
- Salicylates
- Theophylline/Caffeine (both are methylxanthines)
- Methanol
- Ethylene glycol
- Sodium valproate
- Isoniazid
- Carbamazepine

### Common Non-Dialyzable Drugs (AVOID Dialysis)

**AVOID Dialysis**

- **A** — Amphetamines
- **V** — Verapamil
- **O** — Opiates, Organophosphorous
- **I** — Imipramine
- **D** — Digoxin
- **Dialysis** — Diazepam
