  # FDA approved drugs in 2019

  Pearl ID: PM2405

  #flashcards

  **What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "FDA approved drugs in 2019" (PM2405)?**

  ?

  The pearl lists FDA-approved drugs in 2019, organised into specialties based on indication.

  ### I. Autonomic nervous system

  **1. Prabotulinum toxin A -xvfs**  
  Botulinum toxin type A produced from fermentation of *Clostridium botulinum*. It blocks neuromuscular transmission by inhibiting acetylcholine release. Used for temporary improvement in the appearance of moderate to severe glabellar lines associated with corrugator and/or procerus muscle activity in adults.

  **2. Solriamfetol**  
  Dopamine and norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor (DNRI). Used to treat excessive daytime sleepiness in patients with narcolepsy or obstructive sleep apnea (OSA).

  ### II. Central nervous system

  **1. Brexanolone**  
  Neuroactive steroid GABA-A receptor positive modulator, chemically identical to endogenous allopregnanolone. Used to treat postpartum depression (PPD) in adult women.

  **2. Lumateperone tosylate**  
  Atypical antipsychotic indicated for schizophrenia.

  **3. Lemborexant**  
  Orexin receptor antagonist used in the treatment of insomnia.

  **4. Siponimod**  
  Sphingosine-1-phosphate receptor modulator. Binds to S1P receptors 1 and 5 and blocks lymphocyte egress from lymph nodes, reducing peripheral lymphocyte numbers and potentially reducing lymphocyte migration into the CNS. Indicated for relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis (MS).

  **5. Istradefylline**  
  Adenosine receptor antagonist with a xanthine derivative structure. Indicated as adjunctive treatment to levodopa/carbidopa in adults with Parkinson's disease experiencing "off" episodes.

  **6. Cenobamate**  
  Carbamate derivative used in the treatment of partial-onset seizures in adults.

  **7. Ubrogepant**  
  Calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) receptor antagonist. Used for acute treatment of migraine with or without aura.

  ### III. Cardiovascular system

  **1. Caplacizumab-yhdp**  
  von Willebrand factor (vWF)-directed antibody fragment targeting the A1 domain of vWF and inhibiting vWF-platelet interaction, reducing vWF-mediated platelet adhesion and consumption. Indicated for acquired thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (aTTP) in combination with plasma exchange and immunosuppressive therapy.

  **2. Tafamidis meglumine**  
  Selective stabilizer of transthyretin (TTR). Binds TTR at thyroxine-binding sites, stabilizing the tetramer and slowing dissociation into monomers. Used for cardiomyopathy of wild-type or hereditary transthyretin-mediated amyloidosis (ATTR-CM) in adults to reduce cardiovascular-related mortality or hospitalization.

  **3. Ferric maltol**  
  Oral iron replacement product containing 30 mg of iron in a stable ferric state and 201.5 mg of maltol in the form of trimaltol ligand. Used for iron deficiency anemia.

  **4. Luspatercept-aamt**  
  Erythroid maturation agent. Binds several endogenous TGF-β superfamily ligands, diminishing Smad2/3 signalling and promoting maturation and differentiation of erythroid precursors. Indicated for anemia in adults with beta thalassemia who require regular RBC transfusions.

  **5. Crizanlizumab-tmca**  
  Selectin blocker humanized IgG2 kappa monoclonal antibody that binds to P-selectin and blocks interactions with its ligands including P-selectin glycoprotein ligand 1. Indicated to reduce the frequency of vasoocclusive crises in patients with sickle cell disease.

  **6. Voxelotor**  
  Hemoglobin S polymerization inhibitor. Binds HbS and increases the affinity of Hb for oxygen with dose-dependent inhibition of HbS polymerization. Used to treat sickle cell disease.

  ### IV. Respiratory system

  **Combination of elexacaftor / ivacaftor / tezacaftor**  
  Combination of ivacaftor (a CFTR potentiator), tezacaftor, and elexacaftor. Indicated for cystic fibrosis (CF) in patients aged 12 years and older with at least one F508del mutation in the CFTR gene.

  ### V. Gastrointestinal system

  **Tenapanor**  
  Sodium/hydrogen exchanger 3 (NHE3) inhibitor. Local inhibition of NHE3, an apical antiporter in the small intestine and colon responsible for sodium absorption, increases water secretion into the lumen. Used to treat irritable bowel syndrome with constipation (IBS-C).

  ### VI. Endocrine system

  **Romosozumab-aqqg**  
  Humanized monoclonal antibody (IgG2) produced in a mammalian cell line by recombinant DNA technology. Binds and inhibits sclerostin, thereby increasing bone formation and reducing bone resorption. Used to treat osteoporosis in postmenopausal women at high risk of fracture.

  ### VII. Autacoids

  **1. Pitolisant**  
  Antagonist/inverse agonist of the histamine-3 (H3) receptor. Used to treat excessive daytime sleepiness (EDS) in adults with narcolepsy.

  **2. Lasmiditan**  
  Serotonin (5-HT)1F receptor agonist used for acute treatment of migraine with or without aura in adults.

  ### VIII. Antimicrobials

  **1. Triclabendazole**  
  Antihelminthic drug acting against *Fasciola* species. Used to treat fascioliasis in patients 6 years of age and older.

  **2. Combination of imipenem, cilastatin, and relebactam**  
  Combination of imipenem (penem antibacterial), cilastatin (renal dehydropeptidase inhibitor), and relebactam (β-lactamase inhibitor). Approved for complicated urinary tract infections and complicated abdominal infections caused by susceptible Gram-negative bacteria.

  **3. Cefiderocol**  
  Cephalosporin antibacterial with activity against Gram-negative aerobic bacteria. Bactericidal through inhibition of cell-wall biosynthesis by binding penicillin-binding proteins (PBPs). Indicated for complicated urinary tract infections when there are limited or no alternative treatment options.

  **4. Pretomanid**  
  Oral nitroimidazo-oxazine antimycobacterial agent. Used in combination with bedaquiline and linezolid for adults with pulmonary extensively drug-resistant (XDR), treatment-intolerant, or nonresponsive multidrug-resistant (MDR) tuberculosis.

  **5. Lefamulin**  
  Semi-synthetic pleuromutilin antibacterial agent. Inhibits bacterial protein synthesis by interacting with the A and P sites of the peptidyl transferase center in domain V of 23S rRNA of the 50S subunit. In vitro bactericidal against *S. pneumoniae*, *H. influenzae*, and *M. pneumoniae* (including macrolide-resistant strains), and bacteriostatic against *S. aureus* and *S. pyogenes*. Used in community-acquired bacterial pneumonia (CABP).

  ### IX. Anticancer drugs

  **1. Erdafitinib**  
  Kinase inhibitor used for locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma with susceptible FGFR3 or FGFR2 genetic alterations and progression during/following at least one line of prior platinum-containing chemotherapy (including within 12 months of neoadjuvant/adjuvant platinum therapy).

  **2. Enfortumab vedotin-ejfv**  
  Nectin-4-directed antibody and microtubule inhibitor conjugate. Indicated for adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic urothelial cancer who have previously received a PD-1 or PD-L1 inhibitor and platinum-containing chemotherapy in the neoadjuvant/adjuvant, locally advanced, or metastatic setting.

  **3. Darolutamide**  
  Androgen receptor (AR) inhibitor that competitively inhibits androgen binding, AR nuclear translocation, and AR-mediated transcription. Indicated for non-metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer.

  **4. Alpelisib**  
  Kinase inhibitor acting against phosphatidylinositol-3-kinase (PI3K), mainly PI3Kα. Used in combination with fulvestrant for breast cancer.

  **5. Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki**  
  HER2-directed antibody and topoisomerase inhibitor conjugate. Used in adults with unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer after two or more prior anti-HER2-based regimens in the metastatic setting.

  **6. Selinexor**  
  Nuclear export inhibitor that blocks exportin 1 (XPO1), reversibly inhibiting nuclear export of tumor suppressor proteins and oncoproteins such as c-myc and cyclin D1; this leads to accumulation, cell-cycle arrest, and apoptosis. Used for relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (RRMM).

  **7. Polatuzumab vedotin-piiq**  
  CD79b-directed antibody-drug conjugate composed of an IgG1 monoclonal antibody, the anti-mitotic agent MMAE (monomethyl auristatin E), and a cleavable mc-vc-PAB linker. Binding to CD79b leads to internalization, linker cleavage, MMAE release, microtubule binding, inhibition of cell division, and apoptosis. Used with bendamustine and rituximab for relapsed or refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma.

  **8. Zanubrutinib**  
  Kinase inhibitor acting against Bruton's tyrosine kinase (BTK). BTK signalling is involved in B-cell proliferation, trafficking, chemotaxis, and adhesion; inhibition reduces malignant B-cell proliferation and tumor growth. Used in adults with mantle cell lymphoma after at least one prior therapy.

  **9. Fedratinib**  
  Kinase inhibitor acting against wild-type and mutated JAK2 and FLT3. Used for intermediate-2 or high-risk primary or secondary myelofibrosis (including post-polycythemia vera or post-essential thrombocythemia).

  **10. Pexidartinib**  
  Kinase inhibitor mainly targeting CSF1R, KIT, and FLT3 with an internal tandem duplication (ITD) mutation. Used for symptomatic tenosynovial giant cell tumor (TGCT) associated with severe morbidity or functional limitations and not amenable to improvement with surgery.

  **11. Entrectinib**  
  Kinase inhibitor acting against TRK, ROS1, and ALK. Used for adult patients with metastatic NSCLC whose tumors are ROS1-positive and for adult and pediatric patients 12 years and older with solid tumors.

  ### X. Dermatological drugs

  **1. Risankizumab-rzaa**  
  Humanized IgG1 monoclonal antibody binding the p19 subunit of IL-23 and inhibiting interaction with the IL-23 receptor. Indicated for moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults who are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy.

  **2. Trifarotene**  
  Retinoid used for topical treatment of acne vulgaris in patients 9 years and older.

  **3. Afamelanotide**  
  Melanocortin receptor agonist, predominantly binding MC1-R. Used to increase pain-free light exposure in adults with a history of phototoxic reactions from erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP).

  **4. Givosiran**  
  Double-stranded small interfering RNA (siRNA) against ALAS1 mRNA in hepatocytes. GalNAc-linked delivery enables hepatocyte uptake; RNA interference degrades ALAS1 mRNA and reduces circulating aminolevulinic acid (ALA) and porphobilinogen (PBG). Used for acute hepatic porphyria (AHP) in adults.

  ### XI. Dyes and radioactive agents

  **1. Brilliant Blue G ophthalmic solution**  
  Ophthalmic solution containing brilliant blue G dye that selectively stains the internal limiting membrane (ILM), making the membrane easier to visualize for removal.

  **2. Air polymer-type A**  
  Intrauterine foam used as an ultrasound contrast agent. Formed by mixing clear gel with air and sterile purified water, creating echogenic contrast in the fallopian tubes and peritoneal cavity. Indicated for sonohysterosalpingography to assess fallopian-tube patency in women with known or suspected infertility.

  **3. Fluorodopa F-18**  
  Radioactive diagnostic agent used in PET imaging to visualize dopaminergic nerve terminals in the striatum for evaluation of adults with suspected parkinsonian syndromes.

  **4. Ga-68-DOTATOC**  
  Radioactive diagnostic agent consisting of Gallium-68 and octreotide. Binds somatostatin receptor subtype 2 (SSTR2) and is indicated for localization of somatostatin receptor-positive neuroendocrine tumors (NETs).

  ### XII. Other drugs

  **1. Upadacitinib**  
  Janus kinase (JAK) inhibitor used for moderately to severely active rheumatoid arthritis in patients with inadequate response or intolerance to methotrexate.

  **2. Golodirsen**  
  Antisense oligonucleotide that binds exon 53 of dystrophin pre-mRNA. Used in Duchenne muscular dystrophy in patients with a confirmed DMD mutation amenable to exon 53 skipping.

  **3. Brolucizumab-dbll**  
  Recombinant human VEGF inhibitor that binds VEGF-A and inhibits VEGFR-1/VEGFR-2 interaction, suppressing endothelial proliferation, neovascularization, and vascular permeability. Used for neovascular (wet) age-related macular degeneration (AMD).

  **4. Bremelanotide**  
  Melanocortin receptor agonist used in the treatment of generalized hypoactive sexual desire disorder (HSDD), as described in the pearl.
