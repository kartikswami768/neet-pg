# ATT Regimen for TB Patients

Pearl ID: PM1720

#flashcards

**What are the key facts in the Marrow Pearl "ATT Regimen for TB Patients" (PM1720)?**

?

As per the source Pearl's NTEP summary, treatment of TB is based primarily on drug susceptibility testing, irrespective of new, previously treated or recurrent TB cases. The Pearl states that cases sensitive to isoniazid and rifampicin receive the drug-sensitive TB regimen.

### Standard drug-sensitive regimen
| Type of TB case | Intensive phase | Continuation phase |
|---|---|---|
| New and previously treated cases (H and R sensitive) | **2 months HRZE** | **4 months HRE** (the Pearl notes “Only HR as per WHO guidelines”) |

### Drug-resistance classification
- **Mono-resistant (MR):** resistant to one 1st-line anti-TB drug only.
- **Poly-drug resistant (PDR):** resistant to more than one 1st-line anti-TB drug, other than rifampicin and isoniazid.
- **Rifampicin-resistant (RR):** resistant to rifampicin with or without resistance to other drugs; the Pearl states these patients should be managed as MDR-TB.
- **Rifampicin-susceptible, isoniazid-resistant TB (Hr-TB):** resistant to isoniazid but susceptible to rifampicin.
- **Multidrug-resistant (MDR):** resistant to both isoniazid and rifampicin.
- **Pre-XDR:** MDR/RR-TB with additional resistance to any fluoroquinolone.
- **XDR:** MDR-TB with additional resistance to a fluoroquinolone (levofloxacin or moxifloxacin) and at least one additional Group A drug (the Pearl specifies bedaquiline and linezolid as of 2021).

### Standard DR-TB regimens
| Regimen class | Drugs | Comments |
|---|---|---|
| H mono-resistant / poly-drug resistant TB regimen | **Lfx REZ** for 6 months | 6 months, extendable to 9 months; oral drugs only |
| BPaLM regimen (new MDR regimen by WHO) | **Bdq Pa Lzd Mfx** for 6 months | 6 months, extendable to 39 weeks |
| Shorter oral MDR/RR-TB regimen | Intensive: **Lfx Cfz Eto Z E Hh** (6–9); **Bdq** for 4–6 months **or** **Lzd** (4–6) + Lfx Cfz Z E Hh (6–9) + Bdq for 2 months. Continuation: **Lfx Cfz Z E** for 5 months | 9–11 months; oral drugs only. Bdq replaced by injectable amikacin in children <5 years until Bdq is approved for that age group |
| Longer oral M/XDR-TB regimen | **Bdq** (6 months or longer) + Lfx + Lzd + Cfz + Cs | 18–20 months; oral drugs only; used when not eligible for BPaLM or the 9–11 month shorter oral MDR-TB regimen |

### Treatment algorithm
![[images/PM1720_MDR_RR_TB_algorithm.png]]

The source algorithm states:
- New microbiologically confirmed TB is divided according to whether rifampicin resistance is detected.
- With rifampicin resistance, age and eligibility determine BPaLM, the shorter oral MDR/RR-TB regimen, or the longer oral M/XDR-TB regimen.
- With no detected rifampicin resistance: **2 HRZE / 4 HRE**, followed by testing for H resistance; H resistance detected leads to an H mono/poly-drug-resistant TB regimen.
- The source lists BPaLM inclusion/exclusion criteria and eligibility criteria for the 9–11 month regimen in the accompanying algorithm.

### BPaLM inclusion criteria shown in the source algorithm
- New microbiologically confirmed MDR/RR-TB, or probable MDR-TB who failed H mono/poly DR-TB treatment.
- Bedaquiline / Pretomanid / Linezolid exposure <1 month, or >1 month with documented sensitivity.
- QTcF ≤450 ms (male), ≤470 ms (female).
- Non-lactating, or lactating but not breast-feeding.
- Non-pregnant, or pregnant <20/24 weeks of gestation and willing for MTP.

### BPaLM exclusions shown in the source algorithm
- Age <14 years.
- Bedaquiline, Pretomanid, or Linezolid resistance.
- Breast feeding.
- Severe extrapulmonary MDR-TB — CNS, spinal/skeletal, disseminated TB.
- Significant liver dysfunction or cardiac conduction abnormality.

### Eligibility criteria shown for the 9–11 month shorter oral MDR/RR-TB regimen
- Rifampicin resistance detected.
- Fluoroquinolone resistance not detected.
- <1 month exposure to Bedaquiline, Levofloxacin, Clofazimine or Linezolid, or documented susceptibility.
- No extensive TB disease and no severe forms of extrapulmonary MDR-TB.
- Linezolid regimen: pregnant women irrespective of gestational age, InhA mutation and/or KatG mutation.
- Non-lactating, non-pregnant women.
- Pregnant women with <20 or <24 weeks gestation willing for MTP.
