# Dose dependent action of dopamine

| Dose | Infusion rate (mcg/kg/min) | Effects |
|---|---:|---|
| Low | <2 | D1 and D2 stimulation (D1>D2): Increase in renal blood flow and urine output |
| Medium | 2–10 | β1 stimulation: Additional cardiac stimulation |
| High | >10 | α1 stimulation: Vasoconstriction and increase in blood pressure |
