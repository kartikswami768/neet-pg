## Drugs used in acute decompensated heart failure

### Intravenous vasodilators

| Drug | Key point |
|---|---|
| Nitroglycerine | Decrease preload and afterload |
| Sodium nitroprusside | Decrease preload and afterload |
| Nesiritide | Recombinant human BNP |

### Intravenous positive inotropes

| Drug | Key point |
|---|---|
| Dobutamine | β1 receptor-mediated stimulation of cardiac output |
| Dopamine | β1 receptor-mediated stimulation of cardiac output |
| Epinephrine | β1 receptor-mediated stimulation of cardiac output |
| Norepinephrine | β1 receptor-mediated stimulation of cardiac output |
| Enoximone | PDE 3/4 inhibitors |
| Milrinone | PDE 3/4 inhibitors |
| Levosimendan | Combined Ca<sup>2+</sup> sensitizers and PDE3 inhibitors |
| Pimobendan | Combined Ca<sup>2+</sup> sensitizers and PDE3 inhibitors |

### Drugs used for rapid symptom relief

- **Bumetanide, Furosemide, Torasemide** — Loop diuretics used for symptomatic treatment of severe heart failure and acute decompensation.
- **Morphine** — Opioid; to calm the patient and decreases cardiac O<sub>2</sub> consumption.

## Drugs used in chronic heart failure

| Drug | Uses |
|---|---|
| ACE inhibitors | Used as first-line therapy |
| ARBs | Used in case of intolerance to ACE inhibitors |
| Beta-blockers | Used as first-line therapy |
| Spironolactone, Eplerenone | Mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists — first-line drugs for symptomatic heart failure |
| SGLT-2 inhibitors | Used as 1st line therapy — reduces mortality and morbidity |
| Sacubitril/Valsartan | Neprilysin inhibitor/ARB (ARNI) |
| Ivabradine | Inhibitor of I<sub>f</sub> current in the sinoatrial node |
