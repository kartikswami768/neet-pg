# Three way of drainage

### 1) Trabecular Outflow = 90%

# Type of Trabecular meshwork:

iii) Juxta canalicular

> Maximum resistance to flow of aqueous → at Juxta canalicular trabecular meshwork (close to Schlemm’s canal)

### 2) Uveoscleral outflow = 10%

Drain → **Post-ciliary vessels** → choroid → supra choroid space → sclera

### 3) Uveovortex outflow = minimal %

Drain → **Vortex Vein**
