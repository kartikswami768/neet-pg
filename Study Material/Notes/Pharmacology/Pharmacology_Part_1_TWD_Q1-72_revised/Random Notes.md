# High Altitude Sickness
Prophylaxis → Acetazolamide
Treatment → 
	1. Uncomplicated → O2, Dissent
	2. Complicated
		1. HAPE (High altitude Pulmonary Edema)
			1. DOC: Nifedipine
			2. Acetazolamide
			3. PDE 5 Inhibitors
		2. HACE
			1. DOC: Dexamethasone
			2. Furosemide
			3. Acetazolamide


# Electrolyte Imbalance
## Sodium
### Hypernatremia
1. Central DI: Desmopressin
2. Nephrogenic DI: HCTZ
### Hyponatremia
Tolvaptan/Conivaptan

## Potassium
### HyperKalemia
1. Calcium Gluconate (Stabilize cardiac membrane) + Insulin
2. Furosemide
3. Potassium Binding agents
4. Salbutamol/Adr/Steroid
### Hypokalemia
KCl

## Calcium
### HypoCalcemia
### HyperCalcemia
\>12 -≤14 oral BPN
\>14 or any level + symptoms → iv BPN, Furosemide, iv fluids (all three together)
