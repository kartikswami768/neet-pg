### Cardiac Arrest
1. Shockable
	1. VF/pulseless VT
	2. 1st give shock and start CPR
	3. then give epinephrine 
		1. (1:10000 total of 1mg that is 10 ml; intra vascular/intra osseus)
		2. its concentaration is 0.1 mg/ml1
		3. why intraosseus → there is a good chance you will not find the vein in such situation
	4. after epinephrine we can give amiodarone > Lidocaine
2. Non Shockable
	1. Asystole/Pulseless Electrical Activity
	2. we can only give CPR, shock will not work
	3. give Epinephrine

**Amiodarone:**
	**Acute side effect:** Potential for Hear Block
	**Chronic:**
		Please → Pigmentation (Blue skin)
		Check → Corneal Deposit (vortex)
		PFT → Pulmonary Fibrosis
		LFT → Liver Fibrosis
		TFT → Hypo/Hyper
		Least chance of Torsades with amiodarone among all K⁺ channel blocker


**Lidocaine:**
Most Important: ==CNS toxicity==
	- It will block inhibitory pathways of CNS. → CNS excitation (tremors, seizures)
	- Stop the drug at tremors
	- **after Excitation → there will be CNS Depression** (==known as **Post Excitation Depression==**)
- antidote: 20% Intralipid (Lipid Emulsion)

Lidocaine → only anaesthetic that is cardio protective (Because it opens K+ channels)
Bupivacaine → maximum cardio toxic anaesthetic (Ventricualr tachycardia)
Antidote of Bupicaine → same as Lidocaine

### Shock
#### Septic/Neurogenic (Spinal)/Hypovolemic Shock
1. First start IV fluids for 3 hours
2. if Non responsive → Vasopressors (Norepinephrine > Epinephrine (Because Tachycardia is more common with Epinephrine))
3. But if there is already Tachycardia OR patient is not revived with above drugs → **Vasopressin > Phenylephrine (α1 agonist) > Angiotensin II**
	These drugs will not cause tachycardia while increasing BP

#### Anaphylactic Shock
- Low BP and Brochospasm is present
- **we need β 2 stimulation → Epinephrine**
- Dose of Epinephrine → half of cardiac arrest → 0.5 mg (1:1000)
- Intra muscular thigh anterolateral 
- then we can give 
	- **antihistamine/steroid** OR 
	- **Salbuamol (as bronchodilator)**
- How can we give Epinephrine?
	- IM (Best)
	- IV
		- There is risk of arrhythmia (because there is already tachycardia due to anaphylaxis)
		- so we will give low dose (0.1 mg)
		- we will give 1 ml (1:10000)
	- SC
		- action is slow
		- 0.5 mg / 1:1000 /0.5 ml
	- Nasal
		- Self administration
		- 2mg Single Dose Nasal Spray
		- Leave the drug in Superficial Area of nose
		- we can give it even when patient Have Common Cold
		- We can give it in same nostril for 2nd dose → no need to switch nostril
