# 07. Respiratory System
# 1. ANTI-ASTHMA DRUGS
## 1.1 Asthma — pharmacological overview
Asthma is an inflammatory airway disorder associated with:
- Bronchial inflammation
- Bronchoconstriction
- Dyspnea
- Chest tightness/pain
- Wheeze
Anti-asthma drugs are organized into two broad groups:
| Bronchodilators | Anti-inflammatory drugs |
|---|---|
| β2-agonists | Mast-cell stabilizers |
| Anticholinergics | Lipoxygenase inhibitors |
| Theophylline | Leukotriene receptor antagonists |
| Magnesium sulfate | Corticosteroids |
| | Monoclonal antibodies |
The statement that asthma is simply a **"type 1 hypersensitivity reaction"** is an oversimplification.
Asthma is a **heterogeneous chronic inflammatory airway disease**. Type 2 inflammation is important in many patients, particularly allergic/eosinophilic asthma, but not all asthma is explained by a classic IgE-mediated type I hypersensitivity mechanism.
For current treatment, the central pharmacological principle is:
> **Asthma treatment should contain an inhaled corticosteroid (ICS); bronchodilator-only treatment does not adequately address airway inflammation.**
GINA's 2026 strategy remains the current major international reference for asthma management.

The modern acute-exacerbation sequence is more accurately represented as:
```text
Acute asthma exacerbation
 ↓
Assess severity + oxygenation + lung function
 ↓
Inhaled rapid-acting β2-agonist (SABA)
 +
Controlled oxygen when indicated

Systemic corticosteroid for moderate/severe exacerbation
 ↓
If severe:
add inhaled/nebulized ipratropium
 ↓
Reassess response
 ↓
If severe exacerbation persists despite initial treatment:
consider IV magnesium sulfate
 ↓
Escalate to acute/ICU care when indicated
 ↓
Respiratory support / intubation and mechanical ventilation
if impending or established respiratory failure
```
GINA 2026 recommends starting SABA, using controlled oxygen when required, introducing systemic corticosteroids early for moderate/severe exacerbations, and adding ipratropium in severe presentations. IV magnesium sulfate can be considered in severe exacerbations that fail to respond adequately to initial treatment. Antibiotics should **not** be prescribed routinely for asthma exacerbations.
### Aminophylline / theophylline in acute asthma
> **Aminophylline IV — 5 mg/kg**
This dose is retained as **-derived content**, but the therapeutic recommendation is outdated.
** GINA states that IV aminophylline/theophylline should **not be routinely used** for asthma exacerbations because of limited efficacy and a less favorable safety profile.
### Magnesium sulfate
magnesium sulfate as a treatment for status asthmaticus and also describes "inhaler/nebulizations."
- IV magnesium sulfate is **not routine first-line therapy**.
- It can be considered in selected patients with severe exacerbations who fail to respond adequately to initial treatment.
- GINA describes a single **2 g IV infusion over 20 minutes** in appropriate severe cases.
- Nebulized magnesium sulfate does not have a routine role in current adult asthma exacerbation management.
### Antibiotics
> "MC cause of acute severe asthma is chest infection: therefore, antibiotics are started as well"
** Do **not** routinely prescribe antibiotics for an asthma exacerbation. Treat a documented/suspected bacterial infection when clinically indicated rather than treating asthma itself with antibiotics.
### General anesthesia and mechanical ventilation

These are escalation concepts rather than routine asthma pharmacotherapy.
**Clinical principle:** Mechanical ventilation is reserved for respiratory failure or impending respiratory arrest and should be performed in an appropriate critical-care setting. Ketamine has bronchodilator properties and may be useful in selected critically ill patients requiring sedation/intubation; halothane is historically important but is not a routine modern asthma treatment.

# 4. β2-ADRENERGIC AGONISTS
## 4.1 Mechanism of action
### Mechanism
β2-agonists:
1. Stimulate β2 receptors on bronchial smooth muscle.
2. β2 receptors are **Gs-coupled GPCRs**.
3. Gs activates adenylyl cyclase.
4. Intracellular cAMP increases.
5. Bronchial smooth muscle relaxes.
6. Bronchodilation occurs.
The also states:
> cAMP is degraded by PDE4.
and relates PDE inhibition to theophylline.
### Pharmacology
```text
β2 receptor stimulation
 ↓
Gs
 ↓
Adenylyl cyclase
 ↓
↑ cAMP
 ↓
Smooth-muscle relaxation
 ↓
Bronchodilation
```

- Particularly important in acute asthma exacerbations

SABA remains an important acute bronchodilator, but current asthma strategy emphasizes **ICS-containing therapy** rather than SABA-only treatment for chronic asthma management.
## 4.3 LABA — long-acting β2-agonists

- Bambuterol

### Use

## 4.4 Ultra-LABA
### examples
- Indacaterol
- Vilanterol

## 4.5 Formoterol
> "Formoterol: SABA + LABA"
Formoterol is pharmacologically a **LABA**, not a SABA.
The important distinction is:
- **Class:** LABA
- **Onset:** relatively rapid
- **Duration:** long
- Therefore, when combined with an ICS, formoterol can serve as both maintenance and reliever therapy in appropriate asthma regimens.
This is the pharmacological basis of **ICS-formoterol MART/SMART-type strategies**.
GINA 2026 continues to use ICS-formoterol-based reliever strategies in its preferred treatment approach.
## 4.6 Routes
- Inhalational administration
- Oral syrups
- Terbutaline can be given SC
- Salmeterol can be given IV
The **salmeterol IV statement is incorrect** for standard clinical use. Salmeterol is an inhaled LABA.
Terbutaline can be administered by routes including inhalation and, in selected settings, subcutaneous administration; however, inhaled therapy is generally preferred for routine bronchospasm treatment.
## 4.7 Adverse effects of β2-agonists
is:
> **3T-2H**
The listed effects are:
### Tremor
- β2 receptors are present in skeletal muscle.
- β2 stimulation can produce fine tremor.
### Tachycardia
- May occur because of reflex effects, β2-mediated cardiac effects, and some β1 activity at higher systemic concentrations.
### Tachyphylaxis / receptor desensitization
Description: rapid loss of effect due to β2-receptor desensitization.
**Important correction:** Increased corticosteroid dose is not simply a universal method to "overcome tachyphylaxis." Chronic β2-receptor responsiveness is influenced by receptor regulation and corticosteroid effects, but treatment should be guided by the overall asthma regimen rather than simply escalating ICS to counter tachyphylaxis.
### Hypokalemia
β2 stimulation promotes intracellular K+ uptake.
### Hyperglycemia
β2 stimulation can increase hepatic glycogenolysis and contribute to hyperglycemia.
# 5. ANTICHOLINERGIC / ANTIMUSCARINIC BRONCHODILATORS
## 5.1 Mechanism
> "Block the M3 receptor of ACh on the bronchus."
### Core mechanism
Parasympathetic vagal stimulation releases acetylcholine, which activates muscarinic receptors and promotes bronchoconstriction and mucus secretion.
Antimuscarinic drugs reduce cholinergic bronchomotor tone, particularly through blockade of **M3 receptors** on airway smooth muscle and glands.
## 5.2 Classification
| Class | Drug(s) in | Typical role |
|---|---|---|
| SAMA | Ipratropium | Short-acting; acute bronchospasm |
| LAMA | Tiotropium | Long-acting maintenance |
| LAMA | Umeclidinium | Long-acting maintenance |
| LAMA | Aclidinium | Long-acting maintenance |
| Newer LAMA | Revefenacin | Long-acting COPD therapy |
| Newer LAMA | Glycopyrrolate/glycopyrronium | Long-acting COPD therapy |
that LAMAs are used particularly in COPD and that **LAMA + LABA** is highly effective in COPD.
GOLD 2026 continues to recognize LABA, LAMA, and LABA+LAMA as central COPD bronchodilator strategies.
## 5.3 Adverse effects
atropine-like effects:
- Dry eyes
- Dry mouth
- Reduced mucus production
- Constipation
- Urinary retention
These reflect peripheral antimuscarinic activity.
## 5.4 Paradoxical bronchoconstriction
> Ipratropium can cause paradoxical bronchoconstriction by blocking M2 receptors, increasing acetylcholine release.
### Pharmacological explanation
Presynaptic **M2 autoreceptors** normally limit acetylcholine release. Nonselective muscarinic antagonism can interfere with this feedback mechanism.
However, clinically significant paradoxical bronchospasm is an uncommon adverse reaction associated with inhaled bronchodilator formulations and should not be presented as the usual action of ipratropium.
The statement:
> "Tiotropium doesn't cause this, as it does not act on M2"
is an oversimplification. Tiotropium has kinetic receptor selectivity favoring M1/M3 functional effects over M2 at the therapeutic airway level, but the important clinical point is that tiotropium is a LAMA used for maintenance therapy.

# 15. MONOCLONAL ANTIBODIES IN SEVERE ASTHMA
The places biologics under "corticosteroid-resistant asthma."
Biologics are **not simply treatments for "corticosteroid-resistant asthma."**
They are add-on therapies for selected patients with **severe asthma that remains uncontrolled despite optimized standard therapy**, often guided by phenotype/biomarkers such as allergic or eosinophilic inflammation.
GINA 2026 includes anti-IgE, anti-IL-5/IL-5R, anti-IL-4Rα, and anti-TSLP approaches among biologic options for appropriately selected severe asthma.
## 15.1 Omalizumab
###
- Anti-IgE monoclonal antibody
- Listed for corticosteroid-resistant asthma
### Correct mechanism
**Omalizumab binds free IgE**, reducing its interaction with high-affinity FcεRI receptors on mast cells and basophils.
It is used as add-on treatment in selected severe allergic asthma.
## 15.2 Mepolizumab
### Mechanism

### Correct mechanism

# 18. ANTITUSSIVES
## 18.1 General principle
antitussives: drugs that:
- Suppress the cough reflex
- Reduce activity of respiratory/cough centers
- Are mainly used for troublesome **dry/nonproductive cough**
they are contraindicated in productive/infective cough.
### Clinical nuance
Antitussives should be used selectively. Suppressing cough can be undesirable when cough is serving an important mucus-clearance function.
## 18.2 Dextromethorphan
> Blocks the NMDA receptor.
### More complete mechanism
Dextromethorphan is a centrally acting antitussive. Its pharmacology includes **NMDA receptor antagonism** and interaction with sigma-1 receptors.
It is structurally related to opioid compounds but does not act as a conventional opioid analgesic at therapeutic antitussive doses.
### Important safety point
High doses can produce CNS toxicity and, particularly with serotonergic drugs, there is a risk of serotonin toxicity.

High-dose calcium-channel blockers are used in a **small, carefully selected subset of patients with idiopathic/heritable/drug-associated PAH who demonstrate a positive acute pulmonary vasoreactivity test**.
They are **not routine therapy simply because systemic hypertension is present**.
The 2022 ESC/ERS framework emphasizes identifying the small group of vasoreactive PAH patients who may benefit from calcium-channel blockers.

# 28. DRUGS USED IN CYSTIC FIBROSIS
## 28.1 Basic pharmacology
> Defect in CFTR cells on epithelial cells, defective production of Cl− and HCO₃−.
More accurately:
- CF is caused by pathogenic variants **CFTR gene**.
- CFTR is an epithelial chloride and bicarbonate channel/regulator.
- Defective CFTR function alters ion and water transport.
- Airway secretions become dehydrated and viscous.
- The resulting mucus obstruction contributes to recurrent infection and progressive lung disease.

## 29.1 Drugs

- Lumacaftor
- Elexacaftor
- Tezacaftor

### Current classification
| Drug | Class | Core action |
|---|---|---|
| Ivacaftor | Potentiator | Increases opening/probability of responsive CFTR channels at the cell surface |

| Elexacaftor | Corrector | Improves CFTR folding/processing; used in highly effective combinations |
The CF Foundation describes five currently available CFTR-modulator therapies, including ivacaftor, lumacaftor/ivacaftor, tezacaftor/ivacaftor, elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor, and the newer vanzacaftor/tezacaftor/deutivacaftor combination.
## 29.2 Major combinations
### Ivacaftor
Used for specific CFTR variants that are responsive to potentiation.
### Lumacaftor + ivacaftor
- Corrector + potentiator
- Historically important for patients homozygous for F508del.
### Tezacaftor + ivacaftor
- Corrector + potentiator
### Elexacaftor + tezacaftor + ivacaftor
- Two correctors + potentiator
- A highly effective CFTR-modulator combination for many patients with eligible CFTR variants.
FDA records show that the approved population for elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor has expanded over time, including to patients aged ≥2 years with appropriate CFTR variants.

# 32. HIGH-YIELD EXAM PEARLS
## Asthma
- **ICS = cornerstone of chronic asthma pharmacotherapy**
- **LABA must not be used alone in asthma**
- **Formoterol = LABA with rapid onset**
- **SABA = rapid bronchodilation**
- **Ipratropium = SAMA**
- **Tiotropium = LAMA**
- **β2 agonist → Gs → ↑cAMP → bronchodilation**
- β2 agonists can cause:
 - Tremor
 - Tachycardia
 - Hypokalemia
 - Hyperglycemia
- **Theophylline = narrow therapeutic index + major interactions**
- Theophylline toxicity:
 - GI symptoms
 - CNS excitation/seizures
 - Arrhythmias
- **Montelukast/zafirlukast → CysLT1 blockade**
- **Zileuton → 5-lipoxygenase inhibition**
- **Omalizumab → anti-IgE**
- **Mepolizumab/reslizumab → anti-IL-5**
- **Benralizumab → anti-IL-5Rα**
- **Dupilumab → anti-IL-4Rα → IL-4/IL-13 blockade**
- **Tezepelumab → anti-TSLP**
- **Depemokimab → anti-IL-5, long-interval dosing**
## Acute asthma
- First-line bronchodilator: **rapid-acting inhaled β2 agonist**
- Add **ipratropium** in severe exacerbation
- Give **systemic corticosteroid** early in moderate/severe exacerbation
- **IV magnesium sulfate** is an adjunct for selected severe refractory cases
- **Do not routinely use IV aminophylline/theophylline**
- **Do not routinely prescribe antibiotics**
- Respiratory failure → ICU-level escalation and ventilatory support
## COPD
- **LAMA + LABA** is a major bronchodilator combination
- **Roflumilast = PDE4 inhibitor**
- Long-acting bronchodilators are central to COPD maintenance therapy.
## PAH
Remember the three major pathways:
```text
ENDOTHELIN → endothelin receptor antagonists
NO–cGMP → PDE5 inhibitors / sGC stimulator
PROSTACYCLIN → prostacyclin analogues / IP receptor agonist
```
- Bosentan/ambrisentan/macitentan → endothelin antagonists
- Sildenafil/tadalafil → PDE5 inhibitors
- Riociguat → sGC stimulator
- Epoprostenol/treprostinil/iloprost → prostacyclin pathway
- Selexipag → IP receptor agonist
- CCB → only selected vasoreactive PAH
## Cystic fibrosis
- **Ivacaftor = potentiator**
- **Lumacaftor/tezacaftor/elexacaftor = correctors**
- **Elexacaftor + tezacaftor + ivacaftor = highly effective triple-modulator regimen for eligible patients**
- Newer regimen: **vanzacaftor + tezacaftor + deutivacaftor**
