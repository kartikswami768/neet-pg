## Clinical Featrues
1. Recurrent non bilious vomiting (2/3rd week)
2. visible/palpable Olive Shaped mass
3. Visible Peristalsis from left to right
4. **Metabolic Alkalosis with Paradoxical Aciduria**
	- aciduria is there because
	- Hypovolemia will release aldosterone
	- Aldosterone try to excrete potassium and acid
	- Mild Hypokalemia Plus aciduria
- IOC: USG: Length of Pyloric Muscle $\ge$ 16 mm
- Treatment: Ramsted Pyloromyotomy
