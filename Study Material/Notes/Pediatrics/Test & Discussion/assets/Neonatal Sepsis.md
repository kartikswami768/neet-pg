## Diagnosis
Gold Standard: Blood C/S
But it will take time and will not be positive always
### Sepsis Screen
- Very High: Sensitivity/Negative Prediciton Value
- Poor: Specificity

- 4 components:
	1. ANC < 1800 µL
	2. IT (immature to total Neutrophil) ratio > 0.2
	3. µESR > 15 mm/hr
	4. CRP > 1 mg/d;

## Treatment
Supportive + iv antibiotics
- Ampicillin + genta
- If meningitis:
	- add cefotaxime for cns penetration

	> LP is mandatory in LONS 
	> Doesn't matter if they have meningitis or not
	> Because 10% of LONS have meningitis without any meningeal signs/symptoms
	

### Indications of Starting antibodies
LONS: 
- Signs and Symptoms of SEPSIS + postive Sepsis Screen

EONS: 
- 3 Risk Factors or Chorioamniotis
- 2 Risk Factors + Sepsis Screen Positive
- Signs and Symptoms of Sepsis + Sepsis Screen Positive

> Blood Culture is not mandatory to start IV ABx

> Newborn + sepsis $\rightarrow$ No fever But Hypothermia
