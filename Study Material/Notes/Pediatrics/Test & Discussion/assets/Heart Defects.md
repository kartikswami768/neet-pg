# VSD
Most common acyanotic
Most common chd
Types: 
1. Membranous
2. Muscular $\rightarrow$ can close spontaneously because it can contract except **Swiss cheesE**

**Clinical Features:**
1. Pulmonary congestion $\rightarrow$ **More LRTI**
2. Volume overload in LA $\rightarrow$ Left atrium dilation $\rightarrow$ Volume overload in LV $\rightarrow$ **Left ventricle failure**
	1. Manifest as **sweating during breastfeeding**
3. late Cyanosis
	1. Eisenmenger phenomenon: pulmonary htn $\rightarrow$ Rvh $\rightarrow$ right to left shunt
	

**Murmur**
1. **Shunt murmur**: Pansystolic/holosystolic at apex with Thrill
2. **Flow murmur:** due to fluid overload
	1. ejection systolic murmur @ pulmonary area $\rightarrow$ increased blood through pulmonary artery
	2. Diastolic murmur @ mitral area

**CXR:**
-  Cardiomegaly
- Pulmonary plethora
