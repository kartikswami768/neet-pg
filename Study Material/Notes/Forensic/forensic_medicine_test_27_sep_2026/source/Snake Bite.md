## Snake Bit Management
### Not to Do
1. Killing the Snake
2. Cutting
3. Suctioning
4. Torniquett
5. Washing (It will waste time, and will cause muscle movement)

### To do
1. **R**: Reassurance (↓ Fear)
2. **I**: Immobilize Bitten limb
3. **Gh**: Go to Hospital
4. **T**: Tell the Doctor

### What will Doctor Do?
Pressure Bandage @ 55 mmHg
	↓
Compression of Lymphatic Vessels

#### Indication to Start PASV
1. Any Systemic Toxicity (Neuro & Vasculotoxicity)
2. Local Toxicity ↑ with time

- No. 
- There is no systemic Toxicity and local toxicity is samm efor 1 hour
