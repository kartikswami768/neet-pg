## Congenital Diseases of Nose and Septum

### Introduction

|

Development of the nose occurs Between 4th to 8th week of gestation.

|

Neural crest cells are formed from the ectoderm and they are responsible for the

cartilaginous and bony framework of the body

|

They migrate forward and push mesoderm and form pharyngeal arches.

|

In the front neural crest cell form nasal placodes on the both sides and it deepens and

forms the nasal pits.

|

Nasal pits come in the midline and they fuse together to form nasal septum and the

nasal cavity.

|

Maxillary process, frontal process, nasal process, stomatodeum comes in the center and

forms the nose and mouth. Failure of any of these can cause cleft lip or cleft palate.

|

Nasobuccal membrane or Oronasal membrane is present on the posterior side to divide

the nasal cavity from the oral cavity. This membrane disappears at birth. If it doesn't

disappear at birth it leads to choanal atresia.

|

Sometimes the Nasobuccal membrane ossifies and creates bony obstruction between

nasal cavity and oral cavity.
