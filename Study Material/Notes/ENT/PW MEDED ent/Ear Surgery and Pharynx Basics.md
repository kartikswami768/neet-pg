## Facial Nerve and Bell’s Palsy

### Anatomy And Functions Of Facial Nerve

**There are 4 nuclei of the facial nerve:**
1. Motor
2. Parasympathetic preganglionic (Salivatory)
3. Somatic (spinal, trigeminal nucleus)
4. Solitary (for taste)
Motor & Parasympathetic are efferent.
Somatic and Solitary are afferent.
All three except the motor form the intermedius nerve of Wrisberg.
Forehead receives bilateral innervation and is thus saved in supranuclear paralysis.
Emotional movements controlled by thalamonuclear fibers are also preserved.
Bell's palsy is Idiopathic. It is also known as lower motor neuron palsy.

**Clinical Features:**
Onset is sudden.
Patient is unable to close his eye, the eyeball turns up and out (Bell's phenomenon).
Saliva dribbles from the angle of mouth.
Face becomes asymmetrical.
Tears flow down from the eye (epiphora).
Diagnosis of exclusion
**Treatment:**
Medical management with steroids and if it presents in <3 days than Acyclovir. Usually
patients recover within 6-12 weeks.
If in 3 weeks of treatment there are no improvements then we can do
electrophysiological studies.
If >90% degeneration then surgical decompression and if <90% then continue with the
treatment and then do electrophysiological study.

### Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (Hbot)

Treatment modality involving the intermittent inhalation of 100% oxygen in chambers
pressurized above 1 atmosphere absolute (ATA).
HBOT has been used as an adjunctive therapy for sudden sensorineural hearing loss
(SSNHL) as it raises the amount of oxygen in the inner ear by diffusion, which activates
cell metabolism leading to restoration of ionic balance and electrophysiological
functions of cochlea.

![[images/latest-advances-otology-figure-038.jpg]]
![[images/latest-advances-otology-figure-039.jpg]]

**Patient selection criteria for SSNHL:**
Patients with moderate to profound SSNHL (≥ 41 dB) who present within 14 days of
symptom onset should be considered for HBOT.
**Clinical Management:**
The recommended treatment profile consists of 100% O2 at 2.0-2.5 atmospheres
absolute for 90 min daily for 10-20 treatments.
